The **[[Confederacy of the Slayers]]** controls the central continents of the **[[Mortal Plane]]**, the finite fragment of the once-infinite **[[Material Plane]]**. Since the **[[Sealing]]**, the world has been cast in endless night. The land glows only by reflected light from the **[[Catalytic Spheres]]** above the **[[Big Fucking Pit]]**, and by the slow bioluminescence of fungi that grow in the cold and dark.

---

## **Geography of the Shogunates**

### **[[Shogunate of Arcana]]**

The **Shogunate of Arcana** occupies the western continent between **[[The Reach]]** and **[[The Gloam]]**. Its terrain is a mixture of mountain chains and vast mushroom forests that sprawl through the lowlands between the ridges.

The land is pierced by the **Towers of Powder**, crystalline spires constructed from refined minerals such as Sulphur, Clay, and Salt. These towers anchor the **[[Mass Mind]]**, a psionic network that links the continent’s population through shared thought and perception.

- The western range is jagged and metallic, its slopes laced with magnetized veins that hum with psionic energy.
- **[[Mnemon’s Pond]]**, a mercury lake in the eastern foothills, feeds rivers of quicksilver that branch across the continent. These streams catch every trace of light, tracing mirrored veins across the landscape.
- The southern valleys are filled with towering fungal growth that glows faintly in shifting colors.
- The mountains’ interiors contain crystalline chambers and caves that echo psionic vibrations like living conduits.

The Shogunate of Arcana blends mineral and organic terrain. The stone moves as if aware, and the fungal forests pulse faintly.

---

### **[[Shogunate of Religion]]**

The **Shogunate of Religion** spans the northern highlands between **[[The Reach]]** and **[[The Pines]]**. It is a cold, mountainous region where pine forests grow between ridges of gray and gold stone. Wind carves the cliffs into pillars, and snow drifts year-round through its canyons.

Three immense structures dominate the land.

- **[[Angelstone]]** rises at the center of the highlands, a colossal staircase that ascends into the dark sky. The top is lost from sight, disappearing into a ceiling of black clouds.
- **[[Devilstone]]** is a pit in the southern plateau that spirals downward into the **[[Abyss]]**, its walls slick with obsidian and iron.
- **[[The Lighthouse]]** stands upon the northern cliffs. Its vast beam of light can reach across oceans, casting illumination wherever it turns. The area around it is barren and dry, its surface bleached by the light’s constant sweep.

Between these structures, dense forests of pine spread through the valleys, their needles coated in frost that glints with faint silver reflection. The terrain rises in stacked layers, with flat terraces giving way to deep ravines that echo with running water and the sound of wind.

---

### **[[Shogunate of History]]**

The **Shogunate of History** lies east in the Confederacy, between **[[The Pines]]** and **[[The Gloam]]**. Its surface is dominated by endless pinewoods and fungal plains that stretch in alternating belts of life and decay. The forests absorb all sound, and the air glows dimly from the fungus that grows along the trunks and riverbanks.

The **[[Pinewood Expanse]]** covers most of the continent. There is no sunlight here; the only illumination comes from the pale bioluminescent fungus that marks the roads and settlements. Travelers follow these glowing paths, which snake through the woods in intricate spirals.

Three enormous cities rise above the forest. Each is constructed from petrified wood, amber, and glass. The cities are connected by wide, fungus-lined roads that serve both as trade routes and as living networks of light.

At the heart of the continent stands the **[[Acewood Tree]]**, a pine of impossible scale that marks the center of the **[[Godswood Triumvirate]]**. The **[[Kingswood Empire]]**, **[[Queenswood Dominion]]**, and **[[Jackswood Republic]]** form a ring around the tree’s roots. The tree’s sap flows through underground veins, feeding both the forest and the fungus that lights it.

To the south, the pines give way to wetlands where mushrooms grow larger than buildings.

---

### **[[Shogunate of Nature]]**

The **Shogunate of Nature** has always been scattered and tribal. Its people live across isolated islands that circle the Pit and hidden forests within the other shogunates.

- Some islands are volcanic and steep, their slopes veiled in thick jungle.
- Others are coral flats or floating masses of vegetation that drift slowly across the water.

Tribes move constantly, guided by instinct and tradition. Many still maintain ancient shrines to the elements, though most of their ancestors ascended into the Spheres during the **[[Planetary Ascension]]**. The remaining communities serve as stewards of the world’s surviving wilderness.

---

### **[[Shogunate of Investigation]]**

The **Shogunate of Investigation** lies beneath the surface, hidden from the world above. It consists of a web of tunnels, observation vaults, and sealed corridors carved into the crust beneath the inner ocean.

The tunnels are lined with metallic glass and follow natural magnetic fault lines that converge near the Big Fucking Pit. They connect the other Shogunates through unseen passageways that stretch for hundreds of miles.

- Some tunnels intersect with the oldest mines of the Pit, allowing access to its deeper layers.
- Others end in sealed chambers that monitor seismic and psionic fluctuations across the Material Plane.
- Entrances are concealed beneath mountains, ruins, and deep-sea vents.

The environment is cold and silent, lit only by mineral luminescence and mirrored pools of condensed vapor. The tunnels of Investigation are the hidden skeleton of the Confederacy, linking the surface world through unseen paths.
