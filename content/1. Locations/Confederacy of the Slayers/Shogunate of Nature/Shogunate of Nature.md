The **Shogunate of Nature** has always been scattered and tribal. Its people live across isolated islands that circle the **[[Big Fucking Pit]]** and within hidden forests inside the other shogunates. Nature’s territory has no borders or capital; it exists wherever the wilderness endures, guided by the descendants of elemental tribes that have never answered to a central ruler.

The shogunate’s lands are defined by instability and growth. Islands rise and sink with the tides, storms shape new coastlines, and plant life spreads across ruins left by the other shogunates. Many of these islands were once home to the early elemental clans before the **[[Planetary Ascension]]**, when their ancestors merged into the **[[Catalytic Spheres]]** that now hover above the Pit.

---

## **Geography Overview**

The Shogunate of Nature’s territory is composed of countless islands, reefs, and drifting jungles that encircle the southern Inner Ocean.

- The **volcanic isles** are tall and steep, their slopes covered in thick forest and sulfur vents.
- The **coral flats** form sprawling lagoons of bright reef and shallow tide channels.
- The **drifting isles** are masses of tangled vegetation that float slowly across the water, carrying soil, trees, and animal life.
- The **sunken forests** within the mainland shogunates remain hidden under layers of mist, acting as sanctuaries and passageways for Nature’s wandering tribes.

Each island sustains its own ecosystem, shifting with the storms and currents that radiate from the Pit. No two are the same for long, and many vanish beneath the waves for years before returning to the surface.

---

## **Major Locations**

### **[[Isle of Roots]]**
The largest known island in Nature’s domain. Its center is dominated by a single, enormous banyan whose roots extend into the sea, forming channels that connect the island’s lagoons. The tribe that guards it maintains one of the last functioning shrines to the elements of soil and mineral.

---

## **Environmental Notes**

- **Catalytic Interference:** The proximity to the **[[Catalytic Spheres]]** causes frequent shifts in temperature, gravity, and magnetic flow.
- **Seasonless Weather:** The islands experience constant flux; rainfall, heat, and wind follow no pattern.
- **Living Terrain:** Vegetation grows rapidly and unpredictably, often consuming structures within days.
- **Wildlife Migration:** Many species travel between the islands by instinct, maintaining an unbroken cycle of movement around the Pit.

The Shogunate of Nature is not ruled or charted. It moves as the world moves, reshaping itself according to the balance left behind by its ascended ancestors.
