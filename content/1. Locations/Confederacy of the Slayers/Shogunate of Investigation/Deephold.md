**Deephold** is a vast underground chamber located beneath the inner ocean, serving as one of the primary junctions of the **[[Shogunate of Investigation]]**. It lies at the meeting point of three main tunnel systems that connect the western, northern, and eastern continents of the **[[Confederacy of the Slayers]]**.

The central hall is circular and ringed by stone pillars that rise from the black water below. A slow current moves through the chamber, entering from the lower tunnels and exiting through unseen vents in the floor. The air is cold and heavy, filled with the sound of dripping water and the distant hum of the surrounding rock.

No light reaches Deephold from the surface. Its walls are coated in minerals that faintly reflect motion, giving the impression of a shifting horizon beneath the water. Travelers describe the surface as mirror-like, disturbed only by ripples from unseen movements below.

The purpose of Deephold has changed over time. It once served as a meeting place for investigators and couriers, but now only a few sections remain active. Most of its lower levels are sealed by water pressure or cave-ins. The remaining chambers are used as relay points for deep passage and as temporary shelter for travelers crossing the undersea routes.

Deephold remains one of the few places where all three major tunnel systems converge. Its exact depth is unknown, but pressure readings suggest it lies several miles below the ocean floor. Few who descend that far stay long.
