The **Shogunate of Religion** occupies the northernmost continent of the **[[Mortal Plane]]**, bordered by **[[The Reach]]** to the west and **[[The Pines]]** to the east. The land forms a golden triangle of mountain ridges and frozen plateaus that descend toward the southern sea. Its highlands are built around three monumental sites: the **[[Angelstone]]**, the **[[Devilstone]]**, and the **[[Lighthouse]]**, which define both its geography and its faith.

The surface is covered in frost and dusted with metallic sand that reflects faint light across the slopes. Winds sweep down from the peaks, carrying snow through the open valleys. The plateaus are crossed by wide stairways and pilgrimage paths that connect the three sacred landmarks, each rising from a distinct ridge of the same mountain chain.

---

## **Geography Overview**

The Shogunate’s landmass forms a single mountainous wedge pointing north.

- The **western slope** borders **[[The Reach]]**, where volcanic ash mixes with snow to form black ridges.
- The **eastern slope** meets **[[The Pines]]**, where the mountains give way to cold forest and frozen lakes.
- The **southern edge** overlooks the sea, its cliffs cut with tunnels leading toward **[[The First Hallow]]** and the **[[Golden Flats]]** below.
- The **northern rise** holds the **[[Lighthouse]]**, visible from every point on the continent when its light burns.

The region is dry and cold, with strong winds that carve the rock into smooth faces. Golden minerals in the cliffs catch faint light from the horizon, giving the entire land its distinctive hue.

---

## **Major Landmarks**

### **[[Angelstone]]**
Located on the western ridge near **[[The Reach]]**, Angelstone is an unending staircase carved from pale granite. It rises through the clouds and vanishes from sight, lined with statues and hollow shrines. Pilgrims who complete the climb are said to shed their mortal form and ascend as angels, leaving behind only a trail of dust and feathers. The upper reaches are lost in mist and silence, unreachable to all but the most devoted.

---

### **[[Devilstone]]**
Set within the eastern ridge near **[[The Pines]]**, Devilstone is a spiraling chasm that descends into an unseen depth. It serves as both prison and execution ground, where the condemned are cast into the pit to be remade as devils. The walls are covered in carvings of faces and claws that shift in the dim light. At night, the wind carries voices from below, echoing through the valley like distant screams.

---

### **[[Lighthouse]]**
The Lighthouse stands at the northern point of the triangle, where the two ridges converge. It is a towering spire of white alloy and crystal that overlooks the sea and the frozen plains below. The structure once projected a radiant beam across the world, though it now lies dormant. The light chamber remains intact, filled with mirrored plates that hum when struck by wind.

## **Environmental Notes**

- **Golden Plateaus:** Exposed mineral stone that glows faintly in low light.
- **Frozen Valleys:** Narrow basins filled with windborne snow, often used for waystations and shrines.
- **Cliff Sanctuaries:** Carved chambers that overlook the ocean, used by pilgrims for isolation and fasting.
- **Mountain Winds:** Cold drafts that travel in predictable cycles, used to mark time and direction by travelers.

The Shogunate of Religion endures in silence and wind. Many of its temples predate the **[[Sealing]]**, while others were raised after it to preserve what remained of faith. Their foundations are buried in gold stone and frost, and the mountain paths between them are still walked by pilgrims who maintain the old rituals.
