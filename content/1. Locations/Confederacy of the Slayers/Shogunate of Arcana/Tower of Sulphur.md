The **Tower of Sulphur** stands north of **[[Mnemon's Pond]]** in the eastern interior of the **[[Shogunate of Arcana]]**. The land around it is unstable, a cracked plain of scorched soil and mineral ridges veined with dull orange light. Thin streams of gas leak from the ground, carrying the sharp, dry scent of burning sulfur that hangs over the entire region.

The tower rises from a ring of blackened stone, its base surrounded by vents that hiss constantly. Its surface is layered and pitted, formed from cooled volcanic material that seeps from within and hardens in uneven patterns. The outer shell is streaked with yellow residue that flakes away over time, revealing darker, heat-scarred layers beneath.

During psionic surges through the **[[Mass Mind]]**, the tower reacts violently. The ground rumbles, and glowing cracks crawl up its surface before releasing short bursts of gas and light. These reactions often spread to nearby terrain, causing heat ripples that distort the air above the mercury rivers flowing from **[[Mnemon's Pond]]**. Researchers believe the tower acts as both conduit and regulator, venting excess psionic charge from the network into the earth below.

The surrounding region is almost uninhabited. The soil is toxic, and the constant gas flow corrodes exposed metal and weakens stone. Only temporary research camps and sealed relay stations operate here, monitoring pressure cycles and energy resonance. The **Tower of Sulphur** stands as one of Arcana’s most volatile landmarks, a living furnace that links the continent’s psionic network to the restless heat beneath its surface.
