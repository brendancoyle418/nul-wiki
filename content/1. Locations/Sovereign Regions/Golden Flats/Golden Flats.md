The **Golden Flats** are an island of molten metal and salt west of the **[[Mortal Plane]]**, glowing faintly beneath the eternal night. Its surface is made of fine gold dust that glitters like sunlight on water but turns to gray lead when carried beyond the island’s borders. The air tastes of metal and ash, and the sea surrounding it hisses with heat and reflected light. Though small in size, the Flats remain one of the most sacred and contested places in the world.

At its heart rises the **[[Mountain of the First Flame]]**, a volcanic spire that burns endlessly without smoke or shadow. The mountain’s glow reflects across the entire island, lighting the night like a sun that never sets. Around it stretch golden plains, cracked salt flats, and veins of glass that hum when struck by wind. The land is unstable and alive with heat, constantly reshaping itself through erosion and eruption.

Two peoples claim the island. The **magma genasi** and **pendek** inhabit the coasts, living in moving villages built from black glass and cooled ash. They rely on fishing, trade, and forging, crafting tools and ornaments from crystallized goldglass. Inland, the **Divine Ordinance** has taken root — a zealous movement led by the **Lightbringer**, who claims descent from the divine line. The Ordinance believes the world must be made holy again, beginning with the **[[Shogunate of Religion]]**, whose countless faiths they see as heresy. They build gleaming citadels from fused sand and molten metal, their walls reflecting the fire of the First Flame.

The two cultures coexist uneasily, bound by trade and necessity. Pilgrims from both sides travel to the **[[Cathedral of the Sunken Light]]**, a submerged ruin that glows from beneath the molten shallows. The devout say the cathedral contains a reflection of the First Flame itself — a divine fire buried under gold.

The Golden Flats remain in motion, burning quietly beneath the eternal night. Every grain of gold and streak of glass whispers of the fire that never died.

## **Environmental Notes**

- **Gold Dust Fields:** Metallic sand that glows faintly and turns to lead when taken off the island.
- **Glass Shores:** Beaches of fused crystal that hum with pressure from the heat below.
- **Molten Streams:** Narrow rivers of liquid metal that flow between vents and fissures.
- **Ash Coral:** A rare form of petrified reef used by the Pendek to reinforce their boats.
