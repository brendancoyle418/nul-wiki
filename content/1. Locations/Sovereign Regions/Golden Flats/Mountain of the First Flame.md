**The Mountain of the First Flame** rises from the center of the **[[Golden Flats]]**, a black and gold volcanic peak that glows through the eternal night. Its slopes are lined with rivers of molten stone, and the summit burns with a steady pillar of fire known as the **Eternal Maw**. The air around it shimmers with heat, and the wind carries fine gold dust that darkens to lead when removed from the island. The mountain is visible from every coast, its light reflecting across the glassy shores.

Along the coast, a chain of villages built from coral and volcanic glass follows the mountain’s outline. The **[[magma genasi]]** and **[[pendek]]** who live there see the mountain as a divine being, the first fire that brought warmth to the world. Every village faces inward toward the flame, with shrines that keep small fires burning in imitation of the Maw. Offerings of oil, gold, and salt are made daily, and it is believed the mountain breathes through the smoke that drifts from its vents.

The mountain serves as both landmark and faith. Pilgrims travel from distant shores to leave tributes or to test themselves in the heat of the lower slopes. The **[[Divine Ordinance]]** claims the flame marks the world’s last holy site and seeks to spread its light to the rest of the **[[Mortal Plane]]**, while the coastal tribes guard its slopes and protect the old traditions tied to its fire.
