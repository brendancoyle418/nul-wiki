**The Sovereign Regions** are the four elemental territories that encircle the **[[Big Fucking Pit]]**, each once responsible for maintaining a single **Season of the World**. Before the **[[Sealing]]**, these regions rotated slowly around the Pit, and their movement changed the flow of time, weather, and growth across the **[[Mortal Plane]]**. This rotation sustained the **Seasons of Magma, Mist, Ice,** and **Blood**, the four natural phases of creation and decay. When the Sealing halted the world’s motion, the regions stopped in place, freezing the cycle forever. Each now exists as a fixed monument to its lost season, radiating its elemental influence into the night.

---

## **Geography Overview**

The Sovereign Regions form a perfect ring around the Pit, marking the four cardinal directions of the inner sea. Each region retains the qualities of its season, shaping the eternal climate of the world since motion ceased.

- The **northwest** burns under the **[[Golden Flats]]**, seat of the _Season of Magma_.
- The **northeast** lies hidden in fog within the **[[Blackmist Isles]]**, heart of the _Season of Mist_.
- The **southeast** freezes beneath **[[Albeda]]**, realm of the _Season of Ice_.
- The **southwest** bleeds through the living **[[Red Reef]]**, domain of the _Season of Blood_.

Though once bound in rotation, they now remain locked, their energies in conflict rather than harmony. The world no longer breathes or renews, and the night has no end.

---

## **The Four Sovereigns**

### **[[Golden Flats]]** — _Season of Magma_

A desert of gold glass and scorched mineral plains stretching endlessly across the northwest. The air vibrates with heat, and the horizon burns red with reflected light from the Pit. Rivers of molten metal flow beneath the surface, occasionally breaking through as radiant fissures. At its center stands the **[[Mountain of the First Flame]]**, a spire that burns eternally, even in the absence of fuel. Once, when the Flats orbited the world, their fire marked the season of renewal and creation. Now, the heat never ends, and the land around the Flats has turned to slag.

---

### **[[Blackmist Isles]]** — _Season of Mist_

A chain of jagged islands in the northeast, cloaked in perpetual fog that hums faintly in the dark. The mist refracts sound and light, distorting distance and swallowing voices. The air is cool and damp, and nothing ever dries. In the center sits **[[The First Hallow]]**, a massive white figure with a spiral for a face. It neither moves nor decays, but its presence bends the air around it. The Isles once marked the world’s time of rest and reflection, when mists drifted over the land and memory thickened. Now they linger in endless stillness.

---

### **[[Albeda]]** — _Season of Ice_

A massive iceberg anchored in the southeastern sea, glowing faintly beneath the eternal night. Within its frozen core lies **[[Arktara]]**, a sprawling research and entertainment complex where subjects are studied under extreme isolation and cold. Patrons from across the Confederacy use the facility to test prototype spells, weapons, and inventions, often broadcasting the trials for public spectacle. Observation decks overlook frozen testing chambers, and machinery hums beneath the ice as heat vents fill the caverns with mist. Albeda is both a laboratory and a stage, its frozen halls alive with constant experimentation.

---

### **[[Red Reef]]** — _Season of Blood_

A living coastline of coral, bone, and sinew that stretches across the southwest edge of the inner sea. The reef pulses with faint red light, and its waters churn with drifting tissue and spore. The **[[Fleshpits]]** within its ridges vent hot air and chemical gas that feed the Reef’s slow movement. The land smells of salt and decay, and the waves leave stains that never fade. The Reef once marked the time of death and harvest, when the world’s life returned to its roots. Now it remains in unending rot, its season stretched into eternity.

---

## **Environmental Notes**

- **Frozen Rotation:** The regions no longer move, and the world’s cycle of life and death has ceased.
- **Elemental Imbalance:** Each region leaks its power into the sea, creating storms of heat, mist, frost, and blood across the horizon.
- **Eternal Night:** The Pit’s glow is now the only light source strong enough to touch all four regions.
- **Sealed Seasons:** The north burns, the east freezes, the south bleeds, and the west suffocates—forever.
