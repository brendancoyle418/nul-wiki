**Arktara** is a massive underground research facility buried beneath the glaciers of **[[Albeda]]**, run by the **[[Arktara Research Initiative]]**. It stretches for miles under the ice, built from magnetic rail systems that let entire halls and labs move or seal themselves off when needed. Some sections have been abandoned or collapsed, their lights still glowing in the ice where no one goes.

The facility exists to test the limits of mortal life. Every chamber can change temperature, pressure, light, or gravity to study how living beings react under different conditions. Researchers and test subjects live inside the same system, watched at all times by automated overseers that track every result.

Many of Arktara’s projects are funded by Confederacy patrons who use the site to test survival systems, psionic equipment, weapons, and enchantments under stress. The results are recorded, archived, and sometimes broadcast for public research and funding.

Arktara is never quiet. Machines hum through the walls, and whole wings slide past each other in silence. Old hallways reopen into forgotten rooms, and some lower levels still run without staff, carrying out experiments no one remembers starting.
