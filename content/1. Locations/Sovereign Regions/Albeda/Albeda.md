**Albeda** is a frozen iceberg continent located in the southeastern sea of the **[[Mortal Plane]]**, sealed in eternal night. Its surface is a maze of ridges, frozen lakes, and hollow plains that shift and crack with the tides. Beneath the surface lies a vast network of ice tunnels where the temperature drops low enough for light itself to refract and bend. The land hums faintly with the movement of buried glaciers, their slow drift creating a constant low vibration that can be felt through the feet.

The people of Albeda are **ice genasi** and **yeti** who live in circular villages carved directly into the ice. Their homes and halls are lit by phosphorescent frost and heated with captured geothermal vents. They live by fishing through frozen seas, carving tools from bone and crystallized ice, and weaving with threads made from frozen minerals. Despite its isolation, the region maintains limited trade with the Confederacy, exporting rare materials and experimental technologies produced in its interior research station.

---

## **Geography Overview**

Albeda is divided into frozen tiers that rise and fall with the movements of the sea.

- The **upper plains** are endless sheets of snow and ice broken by the ruins of old research outposts.
- The **central chasm** runs through the heart of the continent, filled with shifting fog and the faint blue glow of active vents.
- The **lower caverns** descend for miles beneath the surface, where the air crystallizes into solid flakes of mineral frost. These caves are the only place where **glacite** can form.
- The **southern cliffs** drop sharply into the black water that borders the **[[Gloam]]**, their undersides lined with stalactites of frozen gold and blue light.

Even in stillness, the entire iceberg moves. Its position and shape slowly change over the decades, guided by deep undercurrents and pressure from the sea below.

## **Notable Location**

### **[[Arktara]]**

A research and entertainment facility hollowed into the center of Albeda. Funded by the Confederacy, **Arktara** conducts experiments on stress, survival, and magic in extreme cold. Subjects and staff live under constant observation while trials are broadcast to distant patrons for profit and study. Many prototype spells and artifacts are tested here, often with unpredictable or catastrophic results. The hum of its machines echoes through the surrounding ice, and faint light from its laboratories can sometimes be seen through the frost from miles away.

## **Environmental Notes**

- **Glacite Caves:** Found only deep beneath Albeda, where cold becomes solid. Glacite forms as fine blue dust that glows faintly in darkness. (See **[[Glacite]]**)
- **Frozen Plains:** Windswept ice fields that shift over time, sometimes swallowing entire villages under new snow.
- **Thermal Vents:** Rare pockets of trapped heat used for crafting, cooking, and forging enchanted materials.
- **Frost Tunnels:** Naturally formed passageways that carry sound for miles, often mistaken for voices or movement.

Albeda is the coldest place in the world, yet never silent. Beneath its stillness, the ice breathes, cracks, and remembers every sound that ever passed through it.
