The **Blackmist Isles** are a chain of mist-shrouded islands scattered across the northern sea of the **[[Mortal Plane]]**. The air is thick and wet, filled with a glowing gray vapor that never fully clears. The ground is made of black volcanic stone streaked with veins of pale silver moss, and the tide moves unpredictably, swallowing and revealing land within hours. The sky is the same color as the sea, and the line between the two is often lost entirely.

The isles are home to **mist genasi** and **bogfolk**, who have adapted to the suffocating humidity and near-constant fog. Their villages are built on stilts above dark water, lit by oil lanterns and phosphorescent moss. Most travel is done by small boats that move in silence, guided by the sound of bells and memory. The people trade in salt, fish, and preserved algae, but many avoid venturing far from home. The deeper islands are abandoned, drowned in fog too dense for breath or light to pass through.

At the center of the archipelago stands the **[[First Hallow]]**, a towering white figure with a spiral face. It has sat unmoving since the **[[Sealing]]**, visible only when the mist briefly parts. Those who approach too closely disappear or return as faceless creatures called **hallows**, stripped of memory and voice. Some cults worship the figure as a divine witness to the Sealing, while others see it as a punishment—something left behind to watch. The mists around it hum faintly, vibrating through stone and bone alike.

The Blackmist Isles are quiet but never still. The fog moves as if alive, crawling along the ground and pulsing with the tide. It hides reflections, distorts sound, and remembers everything that touches it.

## **Environmental Notes**

- **Living Fog:** The ever-present mist absorbs light and sound, erasing tracks and distorting memory.
- **The First Hallow:** A massive white figure seated on black rock. The mist thickens near it, and time seems to lose meaning.
- **Tidal Graves:** Sunken ruins that surface briefly during low tide, glowing faintly through the fog.
- **Echo Pools:** Shallow basins of still water that replay the last sounds made nearby.

No light truly reaches the Blackmist Isles. The fog and sea blend together until even direction becomes a matter of faith.
