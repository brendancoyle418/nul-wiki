The **Forbidden Zones** are the most unstable regions of the **[[Mortal Plane]]**, created after the **[[Sealing]]** when the once-infinite **[[Material Plane]]** was forced into a finite form. These regions mark the outer edges of the known world, where the laws of nature weaken and reality begins to break apart. The closer one travels toward the Veil, the less stable the world becomes. Landmarks shift positions, light bends in strange ways, and distance no longer matches direction.

The **[[Confederacy of the Slayers]]** restricts travel within these regions due to their dangerous conditions. Magnetic and psionic fields fluctuate constantly, making maps unreliable and long-range communication impossible. Despite this, the zones are still explored for the ruins and relics buried within them. Many of these structures predate the Sealing and show signs of advanced technology or lost magic.

Each Forbidden Zone is treated as a wound in the world, a place where the Sealing failed to fully settle. Some believe they expand slowly each year, eroding the edges of the Mortal Plane and drawing the land closer to the Veil.

---

## **Major Regions**

### **[[The Reach]]**

A volcanic continent divided by a massive oceanic chasm that drops into the **[[Abyss]]**. The North and South Reach face each other across this split, connected only by rare, unstable bridges of stone and metal. The landscape is dark and jagged, made of black volcanic glass and magnetic ore that attracts lightning. Tremors are constant, and the air smells of ash and sulfur.

Mining outposts from the **[[Shogunate of Arcana]]** cling to the cliffs but are often abandoned after collapses or eruptions. The chasm’s depth is unknown, and explorers who descend rarely return.

---

### **[[The Pines]]**

A frozen forest that stretches across the northeast. The trees are massive and covered in permanent frost, their roots twisting over the remains of older civilizations buried beneath the snow. The forest floor is uneven and hollow in places, concealing networks of ice tunnels and frozen caverns.

The Pines are home to the surviving **Yeti**, **Sasquatch**, **Almas**, **Yowie**, **Pendek**, **Nittevo**, and **Bogfolk** tribes. These groups rarely contact outsiders, living among the ruins of their ancestors. The deeper regions are filled with low fog that scatters sound and hides motion, making navigation nearly impossible.

---

### **[[The Gloam]]**

A continent of living fungus beneath the southern seas. The surface is soft and covered in spore fields that emit blue and green light. Giant mushrooms rise like towers, and the air is filled with drifting dust that glows in the dark. The ground flexes slightly underfoot, and sometimes exhales warm, humid air from beneath the surface.

Below the surface, a dense mycelial network spreads for miles, forming tunnels, chambers, and caverns that shift shape over time. The Gloam’s spores are valuable to the **[[Shogunate of Arcana]]** and **[[Shogunate of History]]** for study, though prolonged exposure can cause disorientation or memory loss.

---

### **[[Big Fucking Pit]]**

The center of the Mortal Plane is marked by the Big Fucking Pit, a massive excavation that has been mined since the first human settlements. The pit stretches so deep that its lower levels have never been reached. Its walls are carved with scaffolds, bridges, and abandoned machinery from generations of miners. Above it float the **[[Catalytic Spheres]]**, remnants of the **[[Planetary Ascension]]** when the **[[Shogunate of Nature]]** left the surface behind.

The air near the Pit vibrates with pressure and magnetic interference. Time feels slower at the bottom, and sound carries too far, echoing long after the source is gone. Mining continues in the upper levels, but lower tunnels are sealed after entire teams vanish or equipment stops working.

## **Environmental Notes**

- **Unstable Terrain:** Mountains rise and collapse in the same year. Rivers change course overnight.
- **Distorted Space:** Distances become unreliable the closer one gets to the Veil. Travel between points is inconsistent.
- **Magnetic Storms:** Psionic interference and high-energy bursts disrupt machines and cause hallucinations.
- **Fungal and Mineral Contamination:** Air and water are often toxic or filled with reactive dust.
- **Lost Time:** Many who enter the zones report missing hours or days, with no memory of travel.

The Forbidden Zones are reminders of what was lost in the Sealing. They mark the limits of what can still exist on the Mortal Plane, and beyond them lies only the Veil.
