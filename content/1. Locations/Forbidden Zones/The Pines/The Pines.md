The **Pines** form the northeastern continent of the **[[Mortal Plane]]**, a land of frozen forests and mist-filled valleys. Every surface is covered in pinewood that grows across ice, stone, and permafrost, stretching from the northern cliffs to the southern shores. A cold sea separates the Pines from the **[[Shogunate of Religion]]**, the **[[Blackmist Isles]]**, and the **[[Shogunate of History]]**, leaving the region isolated but not unreachable. Traders cross by ship or raft to exchange metal and salt for furs, bone tools, and amber resin.

The Pines are silent except for the sound of wind through the trees. Snow falls year-round, and sunlight filters dimly through the dense canopy, casting the forest floor in permanent twilight. The land is home to orc tribes and halfling settlements that live without walls or borders, moving with the seasons and the slow rhythm of the forest.

---

## **Geography Overview**

The Pines are defined by elevation and depth of growth.

- The **northern cliffs** rise sharply above the sea, coated in ice and fog. Pines grow from every crevice, forming jagged green ridges that overlook the frozen ocean.
- The **central valleys** hold the densest forests and the heartlands of the orc tribes. Thick roots twist above the soil, forming bridges and natural paths through the fog.
- The **southern coast** borders the open sea. Mists drift inland, and the forests thin into wetlands where halfling hunters from **[[Halfhall]]** live among the roots and reeds.

Rivers of meltwater wind through the forest, freezing in winter and vanishing underground in summer. Beneath the surface, layers of glowing sap and frost form veins of **glacium**, the amber ice of the Pines.

## **Notable Locations**

### **[[Goblinoid Tribes]]**
The goblinoid tribes are the native people of the Pines. They live in scattered, nomadic villages built from pinewood, hide, and stone, following the migrations of animals and the thawing rivers. The **Yeti**, **Sasquatch**, **Almas**, **Yowie**, **Pendek**, **Nittevo**, and **Bogfolk** tribes each guard a stretch of forest and trade cooperatively with the Confederacy.

### **[[Halfhall]]**
A halfling town along the southern marshlands. Its dwellings are built into the roots of massive trees, connected by wooden paths above the water. The halflings act as guides and traders, moving between orc villages and passing ships. Their hunters are skilled trackers who can move silently through snow and mist.

## **Environmental Notes**

- **Frozen Pines:** Evergreen forests blanketed in snow, their trunks wrapped in frost fungus that glows faintly green in the dark.
- **Glacium Veins:** Formed where sap and frost combine, glacium glows gold beneath the ice and is carved for trade and ornamentation.
- **Mist Rivers:** Shifting banks of fog that follow the forest’s waterways, erasing paths and landmarks.
- **Ice Caverns:** Hollow chambers beneath the high cliffs used as winter shelters and storehouses.

The Pines remain a place apart from the rest of the world, untouched and ungoverned. Its people live by memory and instinct, guided by the quiet strength of the land.
