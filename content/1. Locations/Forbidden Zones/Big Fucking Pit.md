**The Big Fucking Pit** sits at the center of the **[[Mortal Plane]]**, an immense excavation that has been mined by humanity since the dawn of recorded history. The Pit is miles wide and appears bottomless, its walls descending into darkness that no light can fully reach. Layers of exposed stone, metal, and crystal spiral downward in uneven terraces, marking countless generations of digging and collapse.

Unlike most of the world’s great formations, the Pit was not created by divine force or cataclysm. It was carved slowly by human labor, a result of endless mining for metal, fuel, and essence long before the rise of the **[[Confederacy of the Slayers]]**. Over time, it became the foundation of civilization, supplying the materials that built the shogunates and, eventually, the world’s first cities.

Today, the Pit remains active but perilous. Entire settlements cling to its upper edges, while deep-mining colonies operate in near total darkness below. The air grows thicker the farther one descends, filled with magnetic dust and the low hum of the world’s buried heat. The **[[Catalytic Spheres]]** float above the Pit in silent orbit, bathing the surrounding landscape in distorted light and stabilizing the weakened atmosphere left after the **[[Sealing]]**. No one knows how deep the Pit truly runs, but ancient records claim that if one reaches its lowest point, they would find the roots of the Material Plane itself.
