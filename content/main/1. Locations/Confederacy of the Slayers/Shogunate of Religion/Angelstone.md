**Angelstone** is a mountain ascent located in the **[[Shogunate of Religion]]**, west of the **[[Lighthouse]]** and opposite **[[Devilstone]]**. The path begins at the base of a golden plateau and spirals upward for miles, carved into the rock by generations of pilgrims. Each level is lined with weathered shrines, hollow alcoves, and statues worn smooth by wind and frost. The climb becomes steeper the higher it rises, ending in a narrow peak that disappears into the clouds.

The air grows thinner with each step, and the stone underfoot emits a faint hum when touched. Pilgrims say the sound is the voice of the mountain calling them upward. Those who reach the summit are said to ascend, leaving behind their bodies as fine white ash. The ground near the top is dusted with pale residue that glows faintly at night, believed to be all that remains of the faithful who completed the climb.

Angelstone is considered the holiest site in the Shogunate. Many begin the ascent, but few return. Those who do often carry small pieces of the mountain’s stone as relics, said to calm the mind and purify thought. The paths are maintained by monastic orders who live in shelters built into the cliffs, keeping the way clear for the next generation of pilgrims.
