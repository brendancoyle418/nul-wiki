**The Lighthouse** is a towering megastructure in the **[[Shogunate of Religion]]**, standing on the central coast between **[[Angelstone]]** and **[[Devilstone]]**. Built from white stone and brass, the tower rises from a sheer cliff that overlooks the black ocean. Its foundation extends deep into the rock, supported by a network of buried chambers and spiral passages that hum faintly when the light is active.

At the top of the tower is a massive lens of unknown make, able to direct its beam across continents. The light burns steady and gold, piercing the night sky and cutting through storms and fog alike. Where the beam falls, the land brightens briefly, revealing structures, mountains, or ruins that are otherwise lost in darkness. The tower’s machinery draws on power channeled from the surrounding mountains, and its keepers guard the mechanisms closely.

The Lighthouse serves as both temple and instrument. Pilgrims gather at its base to witness the lighting, and its keepers record each movement of the beam as divine instruction. When the light shines inland, it is seen as a sign of blessing. When it turns toward the sea, it marks a warning. The surrounding cliffs are lined with shrines, their bells ringing softly whenever the light passes overhead.
