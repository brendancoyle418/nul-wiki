**Mnemon’s Pond** is a lake of liquid mercury located in the **[[Shogunate of Arcana]]**, between the **[[Tower of Salt]]** and the **[[Tower of Sulphur]]**. The lake’s surface is smooth and metallic, remaining fluid in all temperatures. From it, a network of narrow mercury rivers branches outward like veins, extending across much of the Shogunate.

The pond has no natural inflow or outlet and is believed to be self-generating. Its vapor is dense and reflective, hanging low over the ground. Prolonged exposure to the fumes causes hallucinations, erratic behavior, and eventual death, yet many historical records describe alchemists and psionic scholars deliberately inhaling the vapors in pursuit of revelation. Several breakthroughs in early psionic theory and transmutative alchemy are attributed to visions experienced near the lake’s edge.

Though its exact origin remains unknown, Mnemon’s Pond has long been considered both sacred and dangerous. The mercury rivers are sometimes harvested for use in ritual or experiment, and some remote settlements along their banks maintain strict isolation due to contamination. The pond itself is rarely visited except by licensed arcanists and state researchers.
