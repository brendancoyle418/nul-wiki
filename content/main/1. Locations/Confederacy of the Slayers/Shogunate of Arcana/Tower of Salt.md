The **Tower of Salt** stands on the southern coast of the **[[Shogunate of Arcana]]**, overlooking the shallow plains that separate the continent from the **[[Red Reef]]**. The surrounding land is pale and dry, covered in layers of crystalline sediment that stretch for miles in every direction. The air carries a constant metallic taste, and the ground cracks beneath even the lightest step.

The tower itself is built from pure white mineral, rising like a column of frozen light. Its surface is smooth but unevenly reflective, causing the entire structure to shimmer under the faint illumination of the **[[Catalytic Spheres]]**. Thin deposits of salt crystallize across its base, forming jagged patterns that shift with each tide. The coast nearby floods irregularly, leaving behind pools of mirrored water that dry into concentric rings.

When psionic current flows through the **[[Mass Mind]]**, the Tower of Salt refracts the energy into visible bands that ripple across the plains like heat waves. The air becomes charged with static, and even distant observers can feel a pressure in their chest during high resonance. The tower is also known to emit high-frequency vibrations that interfere with sound, creating long moments of silence across the landscape.

Settlements near the salt plains harvest the mineral crust for trade and construction, though they build at a distance from the tower itself. The structure’s light can be blinding up close, and its energy distorts perception and memory. Despite its dangers, the **Tower of Salt** remains a major calibration point for psionic transmission, anchoring the southern half of Arcana’s network along the edge of the inner sea.
