**Godswood Triumvirate** is the collective term for the three nations that surround the **[[Acewood Tree]]** at the center of the **[[Shogunate of History]]**: the **[[Kingswood Empire]]**, the **[[Queenswood Dominion]]**, and the **[[Jackswood Republic]]**. Together they occupy a vast forest basin of petrified pine, amber rivers, and rootbound cities. The land is cool and damp, covered in mist that glows faintly from the fungus growing along the trunks and roots of ancient trees.

The entire region is threaded with resin veins that flow from the Acewood Tree’s roots. These veins harden over time into amber, forming translucent layers that preserve fossils, ruins, and people alike. Roads follow these amber paths, and light refracts through the ground beneath them.

---

### **[[Kingswood Empire]]**

The **Kingswood Empire** occupies the northern rise of the Triumvirate basin, where the forest thickens around the massive **Kingwood Tree**. The terrain is built on sloped ground, with cities arranged in concentric terraces that descend toward the trunk. The soil is rich in iron and magnetic stone, and the Kingwood itself bears heavy metallic fruit that falls in irregular intervals. The forest canopy here is so dense that sunlight never reaches the ground; instead, the air glows red-orange from the iron dust and the amber light of resin deposits.

The empire’s infrastructure is carved into the roots of the tree, forming layers of vaults and chambers that hold preserved figures in amber. The outer slopes are covered in moss and fungi that shimmer faintly, marking the border between the forest and the kingdom’s farmlands. The environment feels heavy and still, with the constant vibration of metal beneath the soil.

---

### **[[Queenswood Dominion]]**

The **Queenswood Dominion** lies east of the Acewood Tree, built on steep ridges that descend toward the sea. The land is shaped by clusters of thin, towering **Queenwood Trees**, their silver thorns catching what little light filters through the canopy. Settlements are constructed vertically, with wooden platforms and bridges wrapped around the trunks. The terrain is brittle and uneven, the roots binding together to form narrow valleys filled with pools of hardened sap.

When wind moves through the forest, the silver thorns of the Queenwoods hum at different pitches, creating a soft chorus that can be heard across the Dominion. Amber deposits are common along the cliffs, forming smooth walls that reflect the forest’s glow. The coastline is jagged, and the air carries the scent of resin and salt. The forest and the sea seem to merge, their colors blending in pale gold and gray.

---

### **[[Jackswood Republic]]**

The **Jackswood Republic** spans the southern wetlands of the Triumvirate. The land is uneven and soft, with waterlogged soil that supports enormous **Jackwood Trees**. These trees grow copper bulbs that dangle from their branches and reflect light across the flooded ground. The forests are warm and humid, and fog clings to the water’s surface.

Beneath the mud and roots lie deep amber caverns, where capsules of resin preserve figures from older generations. The rivers here flow slow and shallow, dividing into countless channels that twist between root bridges and half-sunken towns. Every surface glints faintly with copper dust, and the air hums from the psionic resonance of the buried amber below.

The **Godswood Triumvirate** forms a continuous ecosystem of forest, root, and resin. Each nation grows from the same source—the roots of the Acewood Tree—and each landscape reflects a different part of its nature: iron, silver, and copper, bound together beneath an endless canopy of pine.
