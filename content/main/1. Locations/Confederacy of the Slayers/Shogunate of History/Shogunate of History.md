The **Shogunate of History** occupies the eastern continent of the **[[Mortal Plane]]**, bordered by **[[The Pines]]** to the northeast and **[[The Gloam]]** to the south. It is a continent of shadowed pinewoods, luminous fungi, and vast ruins buried beneath layers of soil and resin. The air is cool, still, and faintly glowing from the fungus that spreads across bark, roots, and riverbanks.

---

## **Geography Overview**

The continent is divided into overlapping zones of forest and fungus.

- The **northeastern expanse** is covered in dense pinewoods, the ground blanketed with needles and moss. The trees grow close enough to silence the wind. Fungal light traces the main roads in long, unbroken strands.
- The **southern wetlands** are dense with mushrooms that rise higher than buildings, their caps forming bridges across the flooded ground.
- The **central heartland** holds the continent’s great cities, each carved from fossilized wood and amber.
- The **western forests** thin into rolling ground where the pine roots meet the spore fields of the Gloam.

The land shifts constantly as the forest grows over its past. Entire cities have sunk into the resin-rich earth, leaving only smooth amber mounds behind. The glow of the fungus marks the living surface of the continent, expanding and retracting as the seasons change.

---

## **Major Landmarks**

### **[[Pinewood Expanse]]**
An enormous forest that spans most of the continent. Its canopy blocks all direct light, leaving the forest floor in permanent dusk. Fungal patterns wind through the trees, serving as both roads and boundaries for settlements. Travelers move in silence, guided only by the glow beneath their feet.

---

### **[[Acewood Tree]]**
A pine of impossible size standing at the continent’s center. Its roots spread in every direction, forming natural tunnels that connect distant settlements. Around it lies the **[[Godswood Triumvirate]]**, consisting of the **[[Kingswood Empire]]**, **[[Queenswood Dominion]]**, and **[[Jackswood Republic]]**. The Acewood’s sap glows faintly as it flows through the ground, feeding both the pine and the fungal life that depends on it.

---

### **[[Kingswood Empire]]**
The northernmost nation of the Triumvirate. Its cities are carved into the petrified trunks of fallen trees, surrounded by resin channels that carry glowing sap for light and sustenance. The pine canopy above is so dense that only the bioluminescent roads break the darkness.

---

### **[[Queenswood Dominion]]**
Located along the eastern coast, where the forest meets the sea. Amber cliffs rise above the shoreline, and streams of sap pour from the cliffs into the ocean below, hardening into glassy outcroppings. The Dominion’s settlements are built into these cliffs, with chambers extending deep into the amber.

---

### **[[Jackswood Republic]]**
The southernmost of the three nations, bordering the fungal wetlands of the Gloam. Its villages are raised on root platforms above the soft ground, connected by thick fungal bridges. The air is thick with spores, and the glow from the wetlands reflects in the still water, turning night into a pale haze.

---

## **Environmental Notes**

- **Resin Fields:** Layers of hardened sap form smooth amber plains where older structures once stood. Many fossils and artifacts remain suspended inside.
- **Fungal Rivers:** Slow, dark waterways filled with phosphorescent algae that cast steady light along the forest’s edge.
- **Amber Cities:** Built entirely from petrified wood and fossilized resin, their surfaces are translucent and warm to the touch.
- **Silent Woods:** The dense pine canopy absorbs sound, creating areas where even large movements make no echo.
    

Every part of the continent feels alive and preserved at once. The forest remembers every structure built within it, and the fungus grows wherever memory fades.

---

## **Neighboring Regions**

- **[[The Pines]]** – northeastern forestlands of cold air and perpetual twilight.
- **[[The Gloam]]** – southern continent across the inner sea, its fungal storms sometimes reaching History’s southern coast.
- **[[Blackmist Isles]]** – island chain to the northwest, cloaked in fog and home to the **[[First Hallow]]**.
- **[[Albeda]]** – southwestern peninsula containing the cities of Arktara and other coastal settlements.
