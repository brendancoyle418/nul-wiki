The **Shogunate of Investigation** has no land of its own. It exists through a network of tunnels, vaults, and sea routes that run beneath the continents of the **[[Confederacy of the Slayers]]**. These passageways connect the other shogunates through observation chambers, hidden docks, and buried archives. The system is old and constantly changing, expanded by excavation and sealed again when sections collapse or flood.

The shogunate’s structures are built from volcanic stone and reinforced with metal and resin. The air inside is cool and still, with condensation dripping from the ceilings. Most tunnels are lit by mineral veins that glow faintly in the dark, casting reflections across the water channels that run through them.

---

## **Geography Overview**

The tunnels follow the ocean floor and continental crust, branching into three main regions of activity.

- The **western network** runs beneath the **[[Shogunate of Arcana]]**, connecting its magnetic plains to the sea.
- The **central network** lies under the inner ocean and joins the coasts of **[[Religion]]** and **[[History]]**.
- The **eastern routes** link to the pinewood frontier, where they meet the lower archives and flooded shafts near the resin deposits.

Many paths are sealed or misaligned, forming silent corridors filled with water or mineral growth. Navigation depends on sound and touch, since light and magnetic instruments often fail below a certain depth.

---

## **Major Locations**

### **[[Deephold]]**
A vast underground hub beneath the inner ocean. The main chamber contains stone pillars and water channels that flow in a constant circle. It serves as a resting point for travelers moving between networks. Small shrines and workstations line the outer wall, though most are abandoned.

---

### **[[The Underline]]**
A continuous tunnel stretching below the western continents. It acts as the main route between the **[[Shogunate of Arcana]]**, the **[[Shogunate of Religion]]**, and the **[[Shogunate of History]]**. The walls are smooth and wet, with thin streams of cold air that move like tides.

---

### **[[Ironmere Keep]]**
A fortified structure built on the ocean floor. It functions as both a meeting place and a defensive hold for the shogunate. The walls are coated in salt and iron, and its upper decks rise above the waterline through a hidden shaft that connects to the surface. From above, it appears as a lone island tower.

---

## **Environmental Notes**

- **Flooded Chambers:** Many routes have been overtaken by the ocean. Some are completely underwater, while others form half-submerged corridors.
- **Echo Caverns:** Sound carries unevenly. Footsteps fade instantly, but whispers can be heard far away.
- **Mineral Growth:** Crystals and fungus collect along condensation lines, lighting sections of the tunnels in faint color.
- **Collapsed Paths:** Old routes are constantly closing or being re-dug, leaving layers of abandoned chambers beneath the active network.

The Shogunate’s geography is never static. Each generation builds over what came before, sealing off what can no longer be used and digging deeper toward the next layer.

- **[[Shogunate of History]]** – eastern routes under pine forests and resin basins.
- **[[Shogunate of Religion]]** – northern passages beneath mountain plateaus and fortress ridges.
