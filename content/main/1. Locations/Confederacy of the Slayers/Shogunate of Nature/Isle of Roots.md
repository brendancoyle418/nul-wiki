**Isle of Roots** is the largest island in the **[[Shogunate of Nature]]**, located near the southern rim of the **Inner Ocean** where the tides begin to pull toward the **[[Big Fucking Pit]]**. The island is covered by a single sprawling banyan whose roots form bridges, tunnels, and ridges that divide the land into shallow basins. Rainwater collects in these hollows, creating lakes that rise and fall with the sea. The soil is black and wet, and the air carries the smell of sap and salt.

The banyan’s roots extend far into the ocean, anchoring the island and shaping its coasts. Villages are built within the tangled root systems, using resin, bark, and woven leaf to form walkways above the water. The center of the island holds the **Shrine of Gems**, an ancient structure half-buried at the base of the tree. Its walls hum faintly when touched, and the ground around it is veined with crystal where the roots meet the bedrock.

Below the island lies a flooded network of caves that connect to older passages from before the **[[Planetary Ascension]]**. The water glows faintly from mineral light, and the currents change direction without warning. Few tribes travel into the lower tunnels, but those who do bring back clay and stone said to have formed the first **[[Catalytic Spheres]]**. The island remains a center of movement and growth, shifting with the tides yet never sinking.
