The **Red Reef** is an island of living tissue and blood that floats in the southern sea, pulsing gently beneath the eternal night. Its surface is warm, slick, and alive — a mass of muscle, veins, and bone threaded together in constant motion. Thick rivers of blood flow through the ground, branching like arteries before spilling into the sea. The land trembles with each pulse, and the air smells of salt, iron, and something faintly sweet. Beneath the surface, organs shift and re-form, keeping the island alive and slowly growing outward each year.

The inhabitants of the Red Reef are **blood genasi** and **nittevo**, both uniquely adapted to its environment. Blood genasi act as alchemists and priests, harvesting the island’s ichor to create potions, weapons, and healing salves. The nittevo live in the deeper tunnels and along the shore, their pale forms hardened by constant exposure to the living sea. They carve homes and temples directly into the flesh, using blades that cauterize as they cut so the walls heal clean and smooth overnight.

At the center of the island lies **[[The Fleshpit]]**, a vast depression of red stone and sinew that descends into the island’s heart. The pit is lined with teeth-like ridges and covered in twitching tendrils that move with the tide. Heat radiates from below, and the air vibrates with the slow thump of the island’s living core. Many believe the Fleshpit to be a sentient being — a god of blood and growth that keeps the island alive. Offerings of blood and bone are thrown into its depths to keep it calm.

The Red Reef is more creature than land. It digests and rebuilds itself, reshaping its coastline and breathing through vents that open and close like gills. At night, eyes bloom across its surface, watching the sea before melting away with the dawn. The tides around it are red, thick, and slow-moving, and the sound of the island’s heartbeat carries for miles.

## **Environmental Notes**

- **Living Ground:** The flesh shifts and tightens underfoot, reacting to sound and motion.
- **Vein Rivers:** Networks of circulating blood that warm the island and glow faintly underwater.
- **Eye Fields:** Patches of smooth, lidless eyes that reflect light and seem to track movement.
- **Breathing Vents:** Openings that exhale steam and release organic gases in rhythmic intervals.
