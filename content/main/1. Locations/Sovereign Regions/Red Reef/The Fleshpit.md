**The Fleshpit** is a massive biological sinkhole located beneath the southern waters of the **[[Red Reef]]**. It is not a crater of stone, but a living wound in the world’s body. The walls are made of pulsing tissue, veins, and bone, and the pit exhales a constant heat that fills the air with mist and the smell of iron. Streams of red water run from its edge into the sea, staining the coral shelf around it. The pit’s depth is unknown, but its breathing can be felt across the island, shaking the ground in slow rhythm.

The **[[blood genasi]]** and **[[nittevo]]** who inhabit the surrounding reefs believe the Fleshpit to be both origin and ending. They harvest its bile to create medicine, pigments, and ritual fluids used in their ceremonies. Villages cling to the outer ridges, built from coral and hardened sinew, their streets lined with shrines that drip red condensation from the humid air. The inhabitants speak softly near the edge, claiming the pit listens and remembers every sound that enters it.

At the center, enormous tendrils rise and fall with each exhalation. Some claim to have seen eyes open in the walls or figures moving through the tissue like veins of thought. Expeditions sent to study the Fleshpit rarely return. The **[[Shogunate of Arcana]]** once attempted to seal part of it using crystal anchors, but the material dissolved within days. The pit remains open, breathing, and alive, a reminder that the ground beneath the **[[Mortal Plane]]** still has a pulse.
