**Halfhall** is a halfling settlement located in the southern reaches of the **[[Pines]]**, near the fog-covered coast that faces the **[[Shogunate of History]]**. The town is built into the roots of massive pine trees that rise above the marshy ground, with wooden bridges and platforms connecting clusters of homes. The air is cold and damp, filled with the sound of dripping water and the creak of timber in the wind.

Halfhall serves as a meeting point between the orc tribes of the Pines and traders from the Confederacy. Its docks extend into the shallow, mist-covered waters where small boats arrive to exchange food, resin, and glacium for tools, fabric, and salt. Despite its isolation, the settlement is known for its steady trade and hospitality toward outsiders.

The halflings of Halfhall are skilled hunters and trappers, moving silently through snow and fog. Their homes are carved into the roots of the trees, reinforced with frozen resin and lined with moss for warmth. They live in small families or guilds, each maintaining a specific trade route or hunting ground. Though the forest around them shifts and freezes with the seasons, Halfhall remains one of the few stable settlements in the Pines, sustained by careful resource use and strong community ties.
