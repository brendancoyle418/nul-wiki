The **Gloam** lies south of the Confederacy, a vast fungal continent stretching beneath a layer of mist and black water. It is not part of any shogunate and remains one of the most dangerous and least understood regions of the **[[Mortal Plane]]**. The land is soft, humid, and alive, covered entirely in bioluminescent growth that shifts color with the weather. Spores drift constantly through the air, glowing faintly and settling in thick layers that pulse with slow rhythm like a heartbeat.

---

## **Geography Overview**

The Gloam’s terrain is divided between fungal forests, spore plains, and breathing wetlands.

- The **northern shelf** faces the coasts of **[[History]]**, where shallow waters and floating spore mats form a shifting boundary between continents.
- The **central basin** is a dense tangle of colossal mushrooms that rise higher than towers, their caps linked by bridges of hardened mycelium. Beneath them, roots form hollow tunnels filled with mist and stagnant water.
- The **southern deeps** are a region of black pools and sinkholes that open into the Gloam’s underlayer, where heat and pressure feed the fungal core that sustains the entire continent.

The ground exhales in cycles, releasing warm air and spores that drift for miles before settling again. Every surface glows faintly, and the horizon is blurred by mist and bioluminescent dust.

## **Notable Location**

### **[[Gnometown]]**
The only known settlement in the Gloam. Built within the stems and hollows of enormous mushrooms, its tunnels spiral upward in uneven stacks. The gnomes who live there farm the fungus itself for light, food, and building material. Their homes are carved directly into the living tissue, which repairs itself over time, giving the city a shifting, organic appearance. Despite frequent collapses and spore storms, Gnometown remains a stable foothold for mortals in the Gloam.

## **Environmental Notes**

- **Breathing Ground:** The soil expands and contracts slightly, releasing gas that carries spores across great distances.
- **Sporelight:** The fungus emits soft light in shades of blue, purple, and green. Travelers often use it to navigate, though prolonged exposure dulls memory and orientation.
- **Root Tunnels:** Hollow paths run for miles beneath the surface, forming the main travel routes for both gnomes and native creatures.
- **Fungal Storms:** Periodic surges of heat and pressure send clouds of spores into the air, reducing visibility and coating everything in glowing dust.

The Gloam is not dead or alive in any normal sense. It is a continuous organism that grows, decays, and regrows, forming the southern foundation of the world.

## **Neighboring Regions**

- **[[Shogunate of History]]** – northeast across the shallow inner sea, its resin cliffs facing the Gloam’s northern spore plains.
- **[[Albeda]]** – northeastern peninsula whose white shores are faintly dusted by drifting spores from the Gloam.
- **[[Shogunate of Arcana]]** – northwest beyond the Red Reef, its southern rivers carrying trace spores into Mnemon’s Pond.
- **[[Red Reef]]** – northwestern coast of coral and flesh that borders the Gloam’s spore-rich waters.
