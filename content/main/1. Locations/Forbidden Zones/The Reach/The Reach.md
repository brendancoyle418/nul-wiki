The **Reach** lies west of the **[[Shogunate of Arcana]]**, a volcanic continent split in half by a massive oceanic rift that descends into the **[[Abyss]]**. The two halves—North and South Reach—face one another across this chasm, their cliffs jagged and unstable. The ground trembles constantly, and the sky is filled with drifting ash.

The region is made of black volcanic glass, magnetic ore, and molten stone that hums faintly underfoot. Lightning storms are frequent, drawn to the metallic veins that run through the mountains. Many believe the rift was formed during the final war between **giants** and **dragons**, which reached its peak in the last days before the **[[Sealing]]**.

Though much of the Reach is uninhabitable, it remains home to ancient sanctuaries and hidden enclaves of the old races who survived the war.

---

## **Geography Overview**

The Reach is divided by the Abyss into two fractured continents.

- The **northern ridge** rises into steep mountains of iron and ash. This area is colder, with snow mixing with soot, and holds the remnants of the old giant strongholds.
- The **southern ridge** is hotter and more volatile, with lava flows and vents cutting through its valleys. Dragons and kobolds are said to make their homes here.
- The **rift itself** is wide enough to swallow entire cities. Its walls glow red far below, and flying over it is considered suicidal due to constant storms and magnetic distortion.

The Reach is a land of echoes and stone, where thunder rolls through the mountains for hours and the scent of brimstone never fades.

---

## **Major Locations**

### **[[Draconia]]**

A dragon sanctuary built into the cliffs of the southern ridge. The caverns are immense and lined with fused glass from ancient dragonfire. Despite being largely abandoned, the air still carries the heat of living flame. Some dragons are said to sleep within, buried under layers of cooled magma.

### **[[The Burrows]]**

A sprawling network of tunnels and chambers that serve as the primary home of the kobolds. The walls are reinforced with carved stone and scraps of salvaged metal. The tunnels extend deep toward the chasm, where kobolds mine for relics and ore left behind from the war.

### **[[Mountainhome]]**

A vast dwarven fortress carved into the heart of the northern ridge. Its halls stretch for miles, filled with furnaces that never cool. The dwarves trade metal and stone to the **[[Shogunate of Arcana]]**, maintaining their isolation behind heavily fortified gates.

### **[[Giantsthrone]]**

A flat, wind-carved plateau that serves as the last known sanctuary of the giants. The stone there bears their carvings—simple, colossal shapes cut directly into the rock. Storms gather regularly above the Throne, and lightning often strikes in rhythmic patterns believed to echo the voices of the giants themselves.

---

## **Environmental Notes**

- **Volcanic Plains:** The ground is unstable, constantly reshaped by eruptions and quakes.
- **Magnetic Ore:** The mountains hum faintly and interfere with compasses and flight.
- **Ash Storms:** Dust and soot obscure the sky for days at a time, making the land perpetually dim.
- **Molten Rivers:** Lava channels wind through valleys, cooling into obsidian and slag that hardens over ancient battlefields.
