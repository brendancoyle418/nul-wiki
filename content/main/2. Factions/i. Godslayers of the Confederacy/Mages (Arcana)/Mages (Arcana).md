The **Mages** are the architects of magic within the **[[Confederacy of the Slayers]]**, aligned with the **[[Shogunate of Arcana]]**. They study the structure of spells, the movement of energy, and the laws that govern magical systems. Their goal is to refine the use of magic so that it remains a stable and dependable force in the world.

Mages design and maintain the **[[Mass Mind]]**, the psionic network that links the minds of the continent’s people. They operate out of the **[[Towers of Powder]]**, using each tower to focus on a different aspect of magical research. Their experiments range from studying elemental forces to creating enchantments that strengthen the infrastructure of the shogunates.

While many Mages specialize in abstract theory, others apply their work directly by developing new spells, tools, and methods for everyday use. They believe that magic should serve the living world, not dominate it. For them, the art of spellcraft is both scientific and civic—something that sustains the Confederacy as much as it empowers it.

---

## **Roles and Practices**

- Develop new forms of magic and improve existing spells.
- Maintain psionic stability across the **[[Mass Mind]]** network.
- Oversee the Towers of Powder and their elemental research projects.
- Train apprentices in magical law, safety, and discipline.
- Support the other shogunates with enchantments and arcane infrastructure.

Mages value structure and understanding. They see magic as a language that must be learned carefully, not a power to be taken for granted. Their work ensures that the Confederacy’s magic remains organized and safe, even without divine oversight.
