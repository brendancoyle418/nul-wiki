The **Druids** are the caretakers of life within the **[[Confederacy of the Slayers]]**, aligned with the **[[Shogunate of Nature]]**. They protect and restore the natural world, ensuring that the environment and the people living in it remain balanced. In a world without sunlight, the Druids maintain what life still grows beneath the glow of fungi and the light of the **[[Catalytic Spheres]]**.

Druids act as healers, guides, and advisors across the Confederacy. They tend to the land as much as to its people, helping the other shogunates recover from famine, disease, and pollution caused by magical or industrial work. Many also serve as mediators between communities, using their connection to nature and spirit to settle disputes without violence.

The Druids believe that nature is still evolving after the **[[Sealing]]**, adapting to survive without divine light. Their role is to support that change, not resist it. To them, every forest, fungus, and creature is proof that the world can heal itself if given time.

---

## **Roles and Practices**

- Restore damaged environments and purify tainted land or water.
- Serve as medics and field healers within the Confederacy’s armies and cities.
- Act as advisors to leaders of the other shogunates on natural and spiritual matters.
- Commune with spirits that remain from before the **[[Planetary Ascension]]**.
- Maintain the balance between living ecosystems and psionic infrastructure.

Druids value renewal and cooperation. They view the living world as one large organism that must be understood and cared for, not controlled.
