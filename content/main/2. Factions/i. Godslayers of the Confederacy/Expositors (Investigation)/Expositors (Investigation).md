The **Expositors** are the mediators, analysts, and psionic communicators of the **[[Confederacy of the Slayers]]**, aligned with the **[[Shogunate of Investigation]]**. Their purpose is to maintain cooperation between the other shogunates and to ensure that information, research, and resources move smoothly across the Confederacy.

They work through a vast underground network of tunnels and archives that connect every continent. Using psionics, Expositors send messages, coordinate missions, and monitor the Confederacy’s stability. Their role is practical and political: they keep the Godslayers working together and prevent conflict between the orders.

Expositors also investigate disturbances within the **[[Mortal Plane]]**, especially those linked to divine remnants or uncontrolled psionic activity. They view knowledge as a tool for balance and believe that every event has a traceable cause. Their discipline combines communication, diplomacy, and pattern recognition.

---

## **Roles and Practices**

- Maintain the Confederacy’s underground routes and communication tunnels.
- Use psionic methods to transmit thoughts, memories, and sensory data.
- Record and verify reports from all shogunates to prevent misinformation.
- Investigate magical or environmental disruptions caused by the **[[Catalytic Spheres]]**.
- Serve as diplomats and coordinators during inter-shogunate operations.

Expositors value truth and clarity. They see themselves as the Confederacy’s connective tissue, linking the mind of society to its body. To them, understanding the whole is the only way to protect it.
