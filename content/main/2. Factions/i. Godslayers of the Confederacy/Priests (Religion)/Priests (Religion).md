The **Priests** are the spiritual and cultural leaders of the **[[Confederacy of the Slayers]]**, aligned with the **[[Shogunate of Religion]]**. Their duty is to preserve belief, tradition, and moral order in a world without gods. Where divine power once guided creation, the Priests now shape new systems of faith built around humanity’s endurance and cooperation.

They maintain temples, sanctuaries, and archives that serve as centers for education and reflection. Some keep old rituals alive in altered form, while others establish entirely new religions that honor ideals like knowledge, balance, or survival. Priests believe that faith still has a place in the mortal world, not as worship of higher beings but as a means of unity and discipline.

Many serve as counselors and teachers across the shogunates, offering guidance during conflict or hardship. They help communities endure loss, rebuild hope, and find purpose in the long night that followed the **[[Sealing]]**.

---

## **Roles and Practices**

- Maintain temples, monasteries, and sacred sites across the Confederacy.
- Create and adapt belief systems to suit local cultures and needs.
- Serve as teachers, mediators, and advisors to the other shogunates.
- Record oral histories, myths, and philosophies for future generations.
- Conduct rituals that reinforce collective memory and moral cohesion.

Priests value reflection and unity. They see faith not as submission, but as structure—a framework that gives meaning to life in a world that no longer shines with divine light.
