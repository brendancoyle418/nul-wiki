The **Fleshmongers** are a nomadic cult that roams the **[[Red Reef]]**, living off its living ground and the bodies of those who die there. They see the island as sacred and believe the **[[Fleshpit]]** is the heart of all life. Eating from it, or from anything that lives on it, is how they show devotion. To them, flesh is truth.

They travel through the tunnels and coastlines, carving meat from the island’s surface and sealing the wounds with heated bone so it can heal again. When food is scarce, they hunt other travelers—or each other. They carry little besides bone knives and sacks of dried meat, trading what they gather for salt and metal.

The Red Reef itself seems to help them. When they move through its tunnels, the flesh tightens and shifts behind them, closing paths and hiding their trails. The living ground reacts to their presence, opening vents, moving walls, and even swallowing intruders who chase too far. The Fleshmongers use this to ambush and vanish, striking fast and disappearing as the Reef closes around them. Many believe the cult has learned how to “speak” to the island through vibration and ritual cuts, guiding it like a living ally.

They worship the **[[Fleshpit]]** as the “Heart That Eats.” During their gatherings, they throw blood and bone into its depths, believing the Heart answers through the movement of the ground. The oldest among them are called the **Reborn**, said to have been swallowed by the pit and spat back out changed, with skin marked by faint red lines that pulse like veins.

---

## **Beliefs and Practices**

- All flesh is sacred and meant to be consumed.
- The Reef is alive and protects those who feed it.
- The Fleshpit is a god that eats and gives life back.
- Death is a form of nourishment, not an ending.

---

## **Behavior and Traits**

- Move quietly in small groups through tunnels and vents.
- Hunt travelers and scavengers for meat when needed.
- Use the Reef’s shifting terrain for ambush and escape.
- Treat wounds by burning them closed.
- Avoid open water, light, and loud sound.

The Fleshmongers belong to no shogunate. They follow the will of the Reef, living and dying as part of its body.
