The **Hallow Legion** is a group of faceless people that come out of the fog around the **[[First Hallow]]** in the **[[Blackmist Isles]]**. They are the changed ones: people who walked into the mist and came back without faces, names, or memories. The Legion has no leader or language. When the fog moves, they move. When it clears, they stop.

Hallows are pale and bare, shaped like people but empty. They don’t wear clothes or carry tools. They walk slow until they notice movement, then rush forward together. When they reach someone, they grab and pull them into the fog. No one dragged away has ever come back.

They are poor swimmers. When they lose their balance or get caught in waves, they sink fast and wash up later on the beach. After storms, the shores of the **[[Blackmist Isles]]** are often covered in their bodies. Some start moving again when the fog returns, crawling back toward the water.

They make no sound and leave no tracks. Bright light, strong wind, or heat drives them away. They tend to appear near people, noise, or light, especially when someone speaks of the **[[First Hallow]]**.

The **[[Shogunate of Religion]]** says the Legion is a curse left from the Sealing. The **[[Expositors (Investigation)]]** think the fog copies people and brings those copies to life. Nobody knows which is true.

---

## **Behavior and Traits**

- Move silently and never speak.
- Grab and pull living people into the fog.
- Sink easily and often wash ashore.
- Fade when hit by bright light or strong wind.

The Legion leaves behind fog, salt, and nothing else.
