**Body** is the physical world made solid. It is the part of existence that can be touched, weighed, and changed. Everything in the **[[Material Plane]]** is made of body, from stone and metal to plants, water, and flesh. The body gives form to life and provides a place for the **[[Spirit]]** and **[[Soul]]** to exist.

The body follows the same rules for all things. Matter cannot be created or destroyed. It only changes form. Mountains erode into dust, trees decay into soil, and creatures return to the ground when they die. The same material is reused again and again, shaped into new things through natural and alchemical processes.

**[[Alchemy]]** is the study of how this matter reacts and transforms. Alchemists learn the limits of physical law and how to guide materials into new forms. When a forge melts ore into metal or when a seed becomes a tree, the same reactions occur naturally. Alchemy is simply a way to understand and repeat what the world already does on its own.

The body is made from the same elements that exist in the **[[Elemental Planes]]**. Earth forms the bones of the world. Air fills the lungs of living creatures. Fire provides heat and energy. Water carries movement and change. Together, these elements keep the physical world in motion.

In living creatures, the body is a structure that supports the flow of spirit and soul. The spirit gives shape and function. The soul provides thought and will. Without the body, neither could act within the world. When the body stops working, the spirit fades back into the **[[Ethereal Plane]]** and the soul continues into the **[[Astral Plane]]**.

The body is not perfect or permanent. It breaks, heals, and changes. It is the material reflection of everything that exists above and below it. Through the body, life experiences time, pressure, and gravity—the constant forces that define the Material Plane.

The body is what connects all other parts of existence. It is the ground beneath the spirit and the vessel for the soul. It is the foundation of the world, the visible result of every unseen force acting together.
