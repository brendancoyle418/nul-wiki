**Mana Circuits** are a new organ system that first appeared in people born after **Year 10**, following the rise of the **[[Catalytic Spheres]]**. The world had already gone dark in **Year 0**, during the **[[Sealing]]**, when the sun and stars disappeared. Mana circuits developed naturally in the generations that followed, allowing living bodies to draw power directly from the Spheres that now sustain the world.

Mana circuits run through the body like thin glowing lines that follow the paths of blood and nerve tissue. They connect the **[[Body]]**, **[[Spirit]]**, and **[[Soul]]**, letting energy from the Spheres circulate through living matter. This energy flows automatically, guided by emotion and reflex. A person with working circuits might cause sparks when angry, warm air when calm, or resistance to harm when frightened. The reactions are instinctive, not trained, and most people only notice them in moments of stress or focus.

The most visible sign of active mana circuits is how their users see the sky. Since the Sealing, the world has been covered in total darkness. To those without circuits, the sky is black and empty. To those with them, it is filled with stars. Each point of light is a trace of the Spheres’ power, visible only through the connection that the circuits create. The more active the circuits, the brighter and clearer the stars appear. Some see only a few lights, while others see whole constellations that shift with the flow of mana.

Mana circuits grow naturally as part of the body. They are not learned or built but form during early development. **[[Alchemy]]** suggests they emerged as a response to the constant radiation of energy from the Spheres after the Sealing. Children born without them live normally but cannot sense or shape magic. Those born with them can channel power directly, even without training, though this often leaves them drained or disoriented.

The discovery of mana circuits changed how magic is understood. It proved that the ability to use magic is not just spiritual or mental but biological. Every person with circuits acts as a living extension of the Spheres, part of the same cycle that keeps the world alive in its eternal night.
