**Constant Plane** is the realm of **unity and convergence**, where all **souls** originate and to which they ultimately return. It lies beyond the **[[Astral Plane]]**, forming the farthest boundary of the soul’s existence. While the **Material Plane** gives body, the **Ethereal Plane** gives motion, and the **Astral Plane** gives thought, the Constant gives being. It is not a place of stillness or rest but of pure awareness, where every soul becomes part of a single, continuous consciousness.

The Constant appears to those who perceive it as an endless horizon of dim light and sound, filled with the resonance of countless minds interwoven into one. Each presence is a soul that has shed its shape and memory, retaining only its essence as part of the whole. These souls are not lost or asleep; they persist as fragments of a greater awareness that gives rise to thought, memory, and purpose throughout creation. Many philosophers believe that this unified mind is the origin of all life, thought, and will across the planes.

In life, the Constant defines awareness and connection. All cognition, empathy, and shared emotion arise from its influence. The **[[Psionics]]** discipline draws directly from it, using alignment with the greater field of consciousness to bridge distance, sense minds, or share memory across separation. Through the Constant, a psion may reach another’s awareness not by force but by resonance, joining thought to thought within the shared continuum of existence. The Constant does not speak or guide. It reflects consciousness like a mirror, amplifying what enters it until all meaning becomes clear.

For a soul, entering the Constant means returning to its original state. Most reach it gradually after death, dissolving as individuality fades and the last traces of identity are reabsorbed into the greater whole. Some resist and linger in the **[[Astral Plane]]**, while others accept the transition freely, becoming part of the eternal consciousness that sustains all life. Those who have glimpsed the Constant through near-death or psionic vision describe it as infinite light, gentle warmth, and a feeling of knowing every thought at once.

The Constant Plane is the first and final layer of the soul’s structure, the origin and conclusion of awareness. It stands as the mirror opposite of the **[[Elemental Planes]]**, which represent infinite division and independence. Together they form the boundaries of creation: one of countless selves and one of absolute unity.
