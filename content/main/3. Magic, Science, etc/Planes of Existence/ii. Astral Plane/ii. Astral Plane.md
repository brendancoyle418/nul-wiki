The **Astral Plane** is the realm of the **soul**, a boundless void where every living consciousness takes the form of a celestial body. Each soul appears as a star, planet, meteor, or shard of light, its size and color shaped by the strength and nature of the life it once lived. The Astral Plane is not outer space but a sea of memory and awareness, where gravity is replaced by will and thought creates motion. Souls drift endlessly here, circling and colliding in patterns that mirror emotion, belief, and desire.

Every soul follows its own course. Those of steady purpose become planets that move in calm, predictable orbits. Wandering or chaotic souls burn as comets and meteors, crossing through other orbits and leaving trails of fading light. Entire constellations form from like-minded souls, gathering together into radiant clusters that represent shared memory or devotion. Some grow dark and heavy, collapsing into voids that pull other souls toward them until both are forgotten. The **[[Shogunate of History]]** believes these movements define reincarnation: when a soul’s orbit decays, it falls back into the **[[Material Plane]]** and is born again.

The Astral Plane also holds the fragments known as **familiars**, small portions of a soul that can be separated and summoned through magic. When a mage calls a familiar, they are recalling a fragment of their own astral body, shaping it into a living form. These spirits often appear as animals or glowing motes, and when destroyed, they return to the soul’s celestial body to rest until summoned again.

Magic flows freely through the Astral because it is made of pure will. Thought can build or destroy, shape or distort. The untrained who enter often lose themselves within their own projections, unable to tell illusion from reality. Skilled arcanists and mediums use this plane for travel and communication, navigating constellations of thought to locate lost souls or distant familiars.

The Astral Plane is the map of all consciousness, a living record of every soul that has ever existed. It is infinite, radiant, and silent, where memory and identity drift together in the eternal night, waiting to return to form.
