**Ethereal Plane** is the realm of **spirit**, the unseen layer that gives shape and motion to all matter. Where the **[[Astral Plane]]** holds the soul and the **[[Material Plane]]** holds the body, the Ethereal binds the two together. It is the field of living memory that defines what things are and how they act. Every creature, object, and landscape exists here as a faint echo of itself, a reflection made of intent and impression.

The Ethereal is often described as a pale reflection of the Material, where light drifts without clear source and sound carries as distant whispers. Spirits move through it like afterimages of the living, some still bound to their bodies, others fading into formless flow. When a being dies, its spirit passes through the Ethereal as it unravels from matter, carried onward toward the deeper planes where all energy and essence return.

A spirit does not reason or desire as a soul does. It acts through memory, instinct, and residual will. The trace of a craftsman’s hand, the warmth of a hearth, or the pattern of a predator’s hunt can all linger here as motions without thought. These imprints sometimes form **apparitions** or **ghosts**, remnants that repeat fragments of what they once were. Practitioners of **[[Chi]]** learn to sense and influence these echoes, guiding lost spirits onward or restoring harmony when the balance of motion is disturbed.

Travelers who enter the Ethereal Plane find a space without true direction. Distance feels uncertain, and the landscape bends around emotion or intent. A city might appear as a lattice of dim light, a forest as slow-moving mist, and an ocean as a vast, dark current. The Ethereal records every movement of the Material, but its rhythm is slower, like an echo that never fully fades.

The Ethereal Plane leads directly into the **[[Elemental Planes]]**, where spirit becomes substance once more. It is the threshold where pure motion and intent condense into form, shaping the infinite materials and essences that underlie creation. The spirits that pass beyond the Ethereal shed their last traces of identity and merge into elemental being, joining the endless process of renewal that gives the Material its foundation.

The Ethereal connects every layer of existence. It ties body, soul, and matter into one continuous system, linking life to its origins in the elements. Without it, structure could not hold, and the cycle of creation and dissolution would fail. It is the quiet thread that moves between worlds, holding all things in motion and guiding them back to the elements from which they came.
