The **Mortal Plane** is the last surviving world after the **[[Sealing]]**, a broken piece of what was once the **[[Material Plane]]**. It is a dark and limited realm where light only comes from the **[[Catalytic Spheres]]** that hang above the **[[Big Fucking Pit]]**, or from the glow of fungi, molten rock, and psionic energy. The sun is gone, and the sky has stayed black ever since.

Even without sunlight, life has continued. Forests grow by feeding on minerals and faint magical heat. Fungi and coral give off soft light, and rivers of metal reflect what little shines from the Spheres. People have learned to live with the dark, building cities, towers, and networks that keep the world running.

---

## **Structure and Geography**

The Mortal Plane is divided into five main lands known as the **[[Shogunates of the Confederacy]]**. Each one manages a different part of the world and focuses on its own kind of survival.

- **[[Shogunate of Arcana]]** – western continent of crystal mountains, mercury rivers, and psionic networks.
- **[[Shogunate of Religion]]** – northern highlands of snow, gold, and stone, home to the Angelstone, Devilstone, and Lighthouse.
- **[[Shogunate of History]]** – eastern continent of pine and resin forests, where amber cities stand in silence.
- **[[Shogunate of Nature]]** – scattered islands and jungles that circle the Pit and drift on living reefs.
- **[[Shogunate of Investigation]]** – underground tunnels that connect all continents through hidden passages beneath the sea.

Between these regions lie smaller territories such as the **[[Blackmist Isles]]**, **[[Golden Flats]]**, and **[[Red Reef]]**, each shaped by the strange conditions left after the Sealing.

---

## **Life in the Mortal Plane**

Most people live under faint light or none at all. Crops are grown using heat from the ground or bioluminescent plants. Travel is slow and often guided by psionic maps that can sense magnetic and mental signals. Some lands are calm and stable, while others shift and breathe like living things.

Animals have changed as much as the land. Some see by heat, others by sound, and some no longer rely on sight at all. The same is true for people born after the Sealing, many of whom are adapted to the dark and carry traces of magic in their blood.
