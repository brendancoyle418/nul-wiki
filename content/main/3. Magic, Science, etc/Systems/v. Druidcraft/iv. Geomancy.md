**Geomancy** is the study of the land and its movement through the **[[Green Spheres]]**, which govern heat, cold, moisture, and dryness. It represents stability and endurance within **[[Druidcraft]]**, focusing on how natural cycles shape the earth and how life adapts to them. Geomancy is the foundation of environmental balance, the rhythm that keeps the world steady beneath all change.

The Green Spheres maintain the balance of temperature and growth that sustains life. Druids who practice Geomancy learn to guide those forces rather than resist them. They can harden or soften soil, call rain or drought, shift stone, or calm volcanic heat, but only by aligning their intent with the land’s current state. Every act is cooperation between druid and terrain, and forcing imbalance risks collapse.

Geomancers live close to the ground, often in regions where the earth’s movements are slow and deep. They meditate within caves, plateaus, or faultlines, learning to hear the low hum of pressure that runs beneath the surface. Through this practice, they can sense the strain within rock and soil and relieve it through careful release of energy or cooling of heat.

The discipline teaches that the world itself is alive, and its moods are shaped by temperature and density. The land can grow restless, tired, or strong. Geomancers treat it with respect, listening before acting, knowing that even small changes ripple outward across the planet. The Green Spheres provide them the ability to steady that motion and to ensure that the land continues to endure.

To practice Geomancy is to share in the patience of the earth. It is not about shaping the world into something new but allowing it to breathe and remain whole, preserving the balance that every living thing depends on.
