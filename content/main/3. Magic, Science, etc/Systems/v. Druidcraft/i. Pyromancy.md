**Pyromancy** is the elemental study of flame and light through communion with the **[[White Sphere]]**, the source of illumination across the **[[Mortal Plane]]**. It is one of the oldest forms of **[[Druidcraft]]**, centered on heat, renewal, and change. Fire breaks things down so they can be made again, clearing the way for new growth and balance.

The White Sphere gives light to all things, shaping how the world is seen and how life grows. Druids who follow the path of Pyromancy treat flame as a living force with its own voice and purpose. To command fire is to understand its hunger and guide it so that it warms instead of destroys.

Pyromancers often live where the world’s heat is strongest: near volcanoes, lightning fields, and fallen stars. Their rituals involve flame and reflection, using torches, mirrors, and burning salts to open contact with the elemental plane. In ritual, the druid’s body becomes the path for the flame’s power, and their will decides whether it burns or gives life.

Pyromancy is as much about control as creation. Every fire must have a boundary, and a skilled pyromancer knows when to let it fade. Many are called to cleanse after disaster, using their bond with the White Sphere to burn away rot and restore balance.

To study Pyromancy is to understand that fire is change. It destroys and renews in equal measure. Those who lose focus risk being consumed by it, but those who master it find that fire is not only a weapon, but a tool for life.
