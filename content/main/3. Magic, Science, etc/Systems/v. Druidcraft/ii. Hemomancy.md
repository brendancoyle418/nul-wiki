**Hemomancy** is the study of life and motion through the **[[Red Spheres]]**, which control positive and negative energy. It focuses on the constant movement that keeps living things alive, including the flow of blood, air, and lightning. In **[[Druidcraft]]**, Hemomancy represents the world’s heartbeat, the steady rhythm of energy that drives all growth and decay.

The Red Spheres release opposing charges that rise and fall in rhythm. Druids who practice Hemomancy learn to guide that rhythm, using it to restore strength, stop bleeding, or disrupt a creature’s natural flow. It is a practice of harmony and awareness rather than force or domination.

Hemomancers often live near storms, rivers, or fault lines where the land itself moves and shifts. Their rituals use small amounts of blood to connect body and world, marking the druid as part of the same current that flows through all living matter. Through this bond, they can sense and adjust the life around them.

The art requires precision and restraint. Too much interference can drain vitality, while too little leaves the body stagnant. A skilled Hemomancer knows when to apply pressure and when to release it, reading the pulse of both land and creature. To them, blood is not a symbol of pain or sacrifice but proof that life is always in motion.

To study Hemomancy is to understand that life and death are part of the same cycle. The Red Spheres do not favor one over the other. They exist only to keep the current moving.
