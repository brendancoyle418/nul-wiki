**Piety** is the fifth Virtue of **[[Psionics]]**, aligned with the **[[Yellow Spheres]]** and the principle of memory. It governs identity across time, linking past, present, and future into one continuous awareness. Piety is not devotion to a higher being but recognition of continuity within the self and others. It teaches that every moment is part of a larger pattern of thought repeating through the **[[Constant Plane]]**.

The Yellow Spheres regulate temporal flow and reflection. Their resonance allows psions to experience thought outside of linear time, perceiving memory as an active presence rather than a record of what has passed. Through Piety, a psion learns to navigate the layers of awareness that extend across generations and lives, understanding that the field holds every version of every mind simultaneously.

Those who follow this Virtue are known as **Telepaths**. They communicate through shared thought, bridging distance and time by merging portions of consciousness. Telepaths can exchange ideas without words, enter the memories of others, or weave groups of minds into collective focus. Skilled practitioners can also perceive alternate outcomes or remnants of events that have not yet taken form. To them, time is not a straight path but a loop of awareness constantly revisiting itself.

In practice, Piety is cultivated through introspection and connection. A psion learns to open the mind without losing the boundary of self, allowing communication without confusion. The process requires trust and discipline, since every thought exchanged carries emotional weight. Over time, the psion begins to experience continuity of memory between waking and dreaming, between self and others, and even between separate moments in the same place.

In nature, Piety is reflected in the return of seasons, in the memory of rivers that follow their old paths, and in the growth of trees whose rings hold the record of every passing year. To the psion, it represents unity through remembrance, the awareness that thought never disappears but continues to echo through time until it is understood.
