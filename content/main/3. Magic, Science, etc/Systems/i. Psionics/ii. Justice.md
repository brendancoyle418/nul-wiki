**Justice** is the second Virtue of **[[Psionics]]**, aligned with the **[[Red Spheres]]** and the principle of balance. It governs polarity within the field of thought, ensuring that every action, intention, and emotion finds its equal return. Justice is not punishment or reward but the recognition that will always moves in pairs. To act is to create response, and to think is to shape both self and world at once.

The Red Spheres maintain rhythm within the **[[Constant Plane]]**, creating motion through opposition. Their resonance teaches the psion how to balance emotional charge, regulating the flow of energy between self and others. Through Justice, one learns to reflect without absorbing, to release without harm, and to remain steady in the presence of turmoil.

Those who follow this Virtue are known as **Empaths**. They perceive and influence emotion as patterns of resonance within the field. Empaths can sense anger, fear, and joy as physical textures, then smooth or redirect them to restore balance. They often serve as mediators, healers, or stabilizers in psionic networks, keeping the shared field free of distortion.

In practice, Justice begins with emotional awareness. A psion studies their own reactions until they can feel how thought becomes charge and how charge becomes motion. By calming their inner rhythm, they can harmonize the rhythms of others. True mastery lies in neutrality, the ability to maintain equilibrium while surrounded by imbalance.

In nature, Justice is reflected in the swing of tides, the cycle of storms, and the exchange between fire and ash. Every movement finds its counterweight, and through that balance, the system endures. To the psion, this is the essence of Justice: to be the center point within the current, where energy flows equally in all directions and none is lost to chaos.
