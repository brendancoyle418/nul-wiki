**Observation** is the fourth Virtue of **[[Psionics]]**, aligned with the **[[Green Spheres]]** and the principle of awareness. It governs perception within the **[[Constant Plane]]**, allowing a psion to recognize the structure of thought that connects all forms of being. Observation is not simply seeing, but understanding how presence shapes reality. To observe is to witness the pattern behind movement and the quiet between thoughts.

The Green Spheres establish order within the living field of consciousness. Their resonance teaches the psion to sense connections between minds, environments, and events. Through Observation, awareness expands beyond the self, reaching into the relationships that bind thought to matter and matter to will. The psion learns that every object, creature, and motion carries intention, even when it is silent.

Those who follow this Virtue are known as **Clairsentients**. They interpret the subtle impressions left by minds and events, perceiving meaning through sensation rather than sight. Clairsentients can detect presence through memory, feel the remnants of thought imprinted on places or objects, and sense emotional or mental currents in their surroundings. The most skilled can trace causality through these impressions, seeing not just what is, but why it is.

In practice, Observation is developed through stillness and sensitivity. A psion must learn to quiet their own awareness until external patterns can be felt without interference. The goal is not to control what is sensed, but to witness it without distortion. With time, perception becomes continuous, and the psion can move through the world guided by the natural rhythm of connection.

In nature, Observation is reflected in the growth of vines that find their way to light, in animals that sense storms before they form, and in the shifting of leaves that respond to unseen air. To the psion, Observation represents harmony through awareness, the understanding that knowledge is not taken from the world but received from it.
