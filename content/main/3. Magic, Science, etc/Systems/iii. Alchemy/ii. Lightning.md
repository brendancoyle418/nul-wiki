**Lightning** is the second alchemical catalyst, drawn from the **[[Red Spheres]]**. It governs charge, motion, and the transfer of energy between reagents. Where **[[Radiance]]** begins a reaction through ignition, Lightning sustains it by providing direction and flow. It represents the invisible current that connects materials and allows power to move through them in balance.

In alchemy, Lightning reactions are created by building circuits from conductive materials. These pathways allow energy to travel between reagents, energizing or neutralizing them as needed. A well-designed Lightning array keeps a reaction stable by ensuring charge is distributed evenly across all materials. Without this control, reactions may overload, discharge, or collapse before reaching completion.

Copper is the material most closely associated with Lightning. It conducts energy efficiently, resists corrosion, and maintains consistent flow even under stress. Alchemists use copper wires, plates, and sigils to form reaction networks that direct Lightning safely through their experiments. These setups are used in processes such as reanimation, artificial motion, or the activation of complex machinery powered by alchemical current.

Lightning is also vital in natural systems. It animates muscles, triggers reflexes, and maintains rhythm in living beings. The same current that sparks across copper wires flows within the body as motion and impulse. Alchemists see no distinction between the two; all charge, whether artificial or biological, follows the same pattern of energy seeking balance.
