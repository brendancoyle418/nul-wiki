**Mineral** is the fourth alchemical catalyst, drawn from the **[[Green Spheres]]**. It governs structure, stability, and refinement. Where **[[Steam]]** allows matter to flow and merge, Mineral gives that new form its strength and permanence. It represents the tempering of chaos into order, the point at which a reaction becomes durable and complete.

In alchemy, Mineral is used to harden, reinforce, or enhance the internal structure of reagents. Common applications include folding metals repeatedly to increase density or compressing materials under heat and pressure until impurities are forced out. It is also used to strengthen alloys, crystallize compounds, and stabilize volatile mixtures that would otherwise break apart.

Iron is the material most closely tied to Mineral. It is both strong and adaptable, able to be shaped, forged, and reforged without losing its core properties. Alchemists use iron tools, molds, and lattices to direct Mineral energy through reactions that demand endurance. The process is slow and exact, relying on consistent repetition rather than sudden transformation.

In nature, Mineral defines permanence. It is found in the grain of stone, the growth of crystal, and the endurance of bone. It is the force that resists decay, keeping the world from collapsing into dust. To alchemists, Mineral is the measure of completion—proof that a reaction has reached its final, stable form.
