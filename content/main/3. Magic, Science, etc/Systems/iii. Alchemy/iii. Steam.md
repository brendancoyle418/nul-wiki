**Steam** is the third alchemical catalyst, drawn from the **[[Blue Spheres]]**. It governs the transition between solid, liquid, and gas, allowing reagents to change form so they can be combined or refined. Steam represents flexibility and transformation, the point where materials become fluid enough to merge but not unstable enough to break apart.

In alchemy, Steam is used to melt, dissolve, or vaporize substances so that they can be mixed together into a new whole. The process might involve heating two metals until they flow as one liquid, combining them, then allowing the mixture to cool into a stable solid. It is also used to separate impurities, purify compounds, or suspend reagents in temporary states where new reactions can begin.

Silver is the material most closely associated with Steam. It flows easily, carries heat evenly, and returns to form without loss. Alchemists use silver crucibles, mirrors, and channels to maintain control over temperature and transition. The precision of these tools determines whether a reaction stabilizes into something new or collapses into residue and ash.

Steam is also found in natural cycles. Rain, fog, and mist follow the same rhythm of transformation that alchemists seek to master. It represents the balance between containment and release, where matter moves freely but never loses its essence. All things that change, cool, or condense move within the reach of Steam.
