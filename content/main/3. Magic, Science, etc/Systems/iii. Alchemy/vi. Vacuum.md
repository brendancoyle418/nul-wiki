**Vacuum** is the sixth alchemical catalyst, drawn from the **[[Purple Spheres]]**. It governs fusion, nullification, and containment. Vacuum represents stillness, the point where reactions end or merge into one another. While other catalysts promote movement and transformation, Vacuum brings completion by binding, sealing, or erasing reactions that have reached their limit.

In alchemy, Vacuum is used to fuse separate reagents into a single stable form or to stop reactions that have become unstable. It can merge metals that cannot be joined by heat, trap volatile gases, or suspend compounds that continue reacting beyond their intended point. The goal is control and containment, ensuring that the process ends cleanly rather than collapsing.

Lead is the material most closely tied to Vacuum. It is dense, heavy, and resistant to change, making it ideal for vessels, seals, and reaction chambers. Alchemists use lead to absorb stray energy and prevent resonance from spreading, especially when working with unstable mixtures.

Vacuum has no clear parallel in nature but can be observed in balance and silence, such as the instant between breaths or the calm after a storm. To alchemists, it is the pause that defines the reaction, the force that allows two opposites to become one or to cease entirely.
