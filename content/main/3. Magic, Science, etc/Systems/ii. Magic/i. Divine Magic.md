**Divine Magic** is the practice of shaping light and order through the **[[White Sphere]]**, once the sacred channel of the King who ruled before the **[[Sealing]]**. In the age before the Spheres appeared, all Divine power flowed directly from him, passed to priests and knights who served as extensions of his will. When the King vanished, that connection broke, and the White Sphere became the new source through which mortals learned to command the same light.

The **White Sphere** generates the energy that defines structure and illumination across the **[[Mortal Plane]]**, forming the basis of Divine practice. Beginners channel through it to create light, restore vitality, or purify corrupted matter. These acts rely on the Sphere’s power rather than the caster’s own, functioning as a controlled imitation of the King’s original light.

As training continues, mages learn to locate their **Astral Sun** within the **[[Astral Plane]]**, a projection of their own soul that radiates power. Advanced practitioners draw energy directly from this Astral body, bypassing the Spheres entirely. This allows them to create, mend, or destroy using personal control instead of external focus.

Divine Magic specializes in light, purification, creation, and structural reinforcement. It is used in healing, blessing, warding, and in forging constructs of radiant energy. Most religious institutions still rely on the Spheres to channel Divine power, while independent mages prefer direct Astral control for precision and efficiency.
