**Wild Magic** is the manipulation of probability and entropy through the **[[Yellow Spheres]]**, which maintain the five layers of time across the **[[Mortal Plane]]**. It draws strength from disorder and uncertainty, using the natural instability between moments to create power. Practitioners of Wild Magic do not control time but disturb it, pulling from the tension between what once was, what is, and what may be. Each spell reshapes the balance of cause and effect, often in ways even the caster cannot predict.

The **Yellow Spheres** govern the distant past, past, present, future, and distant future, each orbiting the others in constant motion. Their instability produces entropy, the slow unraveling of order that allows change to exist. Wild mages learn to harness this movement, drawing potential from the friction of time itself. To cast through these Spheres is to invite uncertainty; even a small alteration can echo across multiple layers of reality.

A wild mage’s **Astral Body** takes the form of a **Nebula** within the **[[Astral Plane]]**, a vast field of gas and light where stars are born and destroyed in endless sequence. The nebula represents potential without structure, creation and collapse occurring at once. Experienced mages can draw power from their own Astral Nebula, using its shifting state to influence probability and motion in the present.

Wild Magic is dangerous and rarely stable. It can alter outcomes, invert natural laws, or create phenomena that cannot be repeated. It is most often used when no other form of casting can achieve the desired result. To practice it is to accept risk as part of the process, understanding that entropy is not destruction but the source of all change.
