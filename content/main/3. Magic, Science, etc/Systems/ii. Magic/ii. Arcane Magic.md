**Arcane Magic** is the structured control of energy through the **[[Red Spheres]]**, which regulate positive and negative charge across the **[[Mortal Plane]]**. It is the foundation of modern magical study, built on principles of precision, balance, and repetition. Before the **[[Sealing]]**, the King’s scholars formalized these methods into the world’s first unified casting system, allowing mortals to reproduce stable results without divine intervention.

The **Red Spheres** act as the training focus for apprentices. One sphere provides positive charge to begin a spell, and the other draws it back to end it. This flow of charge and return prevents magical overload and defines the arcane process. All arcane spells are built on this circuit, whether they manipulate matter, motion, or raw energy.

An arcane practitioner’s **Astral Body** manifests as an **Astral Planet** within the **[[Astral Plane]]**, a celestial form that stores and releases energy in regular cycles. Beginners depend on the Spheres to stabilize this orbit, while advanced mages learn to regulate it themselves, drawing energy directly from their Astral Planet. When this control is mastered, the caster no longer relies on the Spheres to maintain charge.

Arcane Magic emphasizes consistency and safety. Each spell has a start, a resolution, and a return to balance. It is used in the operation of magical devices, the creation of sigils, the study of planar energy, and the design of large-scale enchantments. Its principles are taught in most academies across the shogunates, where casting is treated as engineering rather than faith.
