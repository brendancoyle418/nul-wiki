The **Goblinoid Tribes** of [[The Pines]] are nomadic clans that live deep within the frozen forests and misted valleys of the northeastern continent. They are the oldest inhabitants of the region, predating both orc migration and halfling settlement. Each tribe is adapted to its terrain—snow, ice, swamp, or mountain—and all share the same belief that the Pines themselves are alive and watching.

They travel with the seasons, hunting frost elk and glacier whales, gathering pine resin, and trading amber and bone crafts with halfling caravans from **[[Halfhall]]**. Each tribe maintains its own dialect and customs but gathers once each decade at the **Root Convergence**, a meeting held beneath the oldest pine in the forest to renew peace and territory lines.

---

## **The Tribes**

- **Yeti Tribe** – Mountain dwellers who live along the frozen cliffs in the north. They are tall and thickly furred, using bone tools and snow-covered hides to camouflage themselves from predators and outsiders. Their villages are built into caves warmed by underground vents.
- **Sasquatch Tribe** – Found in the central valleys where the snow never fully melts. They are powerful woodcutters and builders, known for shaping massive totems and bridges from living trees. Their hunters can move through blizzards without leaving tracks.
- **Almas Tribe** – Inhabit the fog-shrouded western pines near the sea. They are smaller and leaner than the mountain tribes, with pale eyes that see through mist. They serve as messengers and guides, leading travelers safely through the dense forests.
- **Yowie Tribe** – Live along the southern coasts where the Pines meet the sea. They are skilled swimmers and fishers, building floating camps from pine rafts. They are the most open to trade and are known for crafting tools from glacium, the golden ice of the Pines.
- **Pendek Tribe** – Short and fast, they dwell near the border between forest and marsh. Pendek scouts move in silence and act as mediators between goblinoids and the halflings of **[[Halfhall]]**. They often serve as trackers for Confederacy expeditions.
- **Nittevo Tribe** – Dwell in the deep tunnels beneath the forest floor. They are pale-skinned and sharp-toothed, relying on ambush and night hunting. Despite their appearance, they are fiercely loyal to their kin and often protect surface tribes from intruders.
- **Bogfolk Tribe** – Found in the southern wetlands where the Pines thin into swamp. They are amphibious and live in reed huts that rise and fall with the tides. They harvest algae, frogs, and fungus for trade and medicine.
    

---

## **Culture and Customs**

The goblinoids treat the forest as a single living entity. They bury their dead under pine roots so that their bodies feed the trees, believing that the forest remembers every life given to it. They do not build permanent cities, instead leaving behind wooden totems and carvings that mark ancestral paths through the woods.

Every tribe is bound by an unspoken rule: no fire larger than a torch is allowed beneath the trees. Fire, they believe, is the only thing that can kill the Pines themselves.
