**Divine Ordinance**

The **Divine Ordinance** is a militant faith based in the **[[Golden Flats]]**, centered around the belief that the world must be made pure again through fire. Its followers claim that the light of creation was never truly lost during the **[[Sealing]]**, only buried beneath corruption. The movement is led by the **[[Lightbringer]]**, who claims descent from the last divine line and serves as both ruler and prophet.

The Ordinance believes the First Flame still burns at the heart of the **[[Mountain of the First Flame]]**, and that it can be used to restore the world. To them, every impurity—spiritual, physical, or magical—must be burned away before the flame can shine freely again. Their motto is simple: _“Only what endures the fire is true.”_

The group rose to power on the molten plains of the Golden Flats, building citadels of fused sand and goldglass that reflect the mountain’s glow. From there they send out preachers, soldiers, and craftsmen to convert or conquer. The **[[Shogunate of Religion]]** is their primary target, accused of fracturing faith into countless false sects.

---

## **Beliefs**

- The world was not ended by the Sealing, only darkened.
- Fire is the source of life and truth, and must be used to test all things.
- The divine never died; it waits to be reborn through flame and obedience.
- The weak and unworthy are not to be hated but purified through trial.

Followers of the Ordinance wear simple ash-gray clothing marked with streaks of gold dust. They do not worship idols or images, believing that divine form exists only in light and heat. Their rituals involve controlled burns, molten forges, and chants spoken over glowing metal until it cools.

---

## **Organization**

The Divine Ordinance is built like an army.

- The **Lightbringer** leads as both spiritual and political head.
- The **Anointed Guard** serve as knights, smiths, and enforcers.
- The **Kindlers** are priests and preachers who travel between citadels.
- The **Ashbound** are laborers and converts who maintain forges and build new settlements.

Their capital, called the **Citadel of Dawn**, stands near the molten slopes of the **[[Mountain of the First Flame]]**. Its outer walls are black glass, and its inner halls glow gold from the constant fire of the forges.

---

## **Relations**

The Ordinance trades cautiously with the **magma genasi** and **pendek**, who depend on their forges but resent their zeal. Pilgrims from both sides meet uneasily at the **[[Cathedral of the Sunken Light]]**, where they pray to the reflection of the First Flame. The **[[Shogunate of Religion]]** views the group as heretical, while the **[[Shogunate of Arcana]]** monitors them for signs of magical experimentation near the mountain.
