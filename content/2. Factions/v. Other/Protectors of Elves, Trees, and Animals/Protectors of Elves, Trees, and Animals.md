The **Protectors of Elves, Trees, and Animals** are a radical druidic movement that formed in the frozen forests of **[[The Pines]]**. They claim to defend nature from exploitation, but their methods are violent and unpredictable. Most view them as ecoterrorists. Their members are mostly elves, druids, and beastfolk who broke away from the **[[Shogunate of Nature]]**, believing that it has become too passive and bureaucratic to protect the wild.

The Protectors see animals and forests as equals, both capable of suffering and deserving of freedom. They believe that nature itself has a will and that mortals exist to serve it, not study it. To them, civilization is a sickness, and any harm done to the natural world must be answered with force.

---

## **Beliefs and Goals**

- Animals are sacred beings, not resources.
- Magic and science are tools of cruelty when used against nature.
- The **[[Arktara Research Initiative]]** is an abomination that must be destroyed.
- The natural world must be left untouched, even if that means human extinction.
- Elves and druids must act as the planet’s defenders, not its caretakers.

The Protectors’ hatred of the **[[Arktara Research Initiative]]** defines much of their activity. They view the facility’s animal testing, environmental manipulation, and psionic conditioning experiments as unforgivable crimes. Attacks on Arktara supply lines, personnel, and communication routes are common. They have also sabotaged expeditions from the **[[Shogunate of Arcana]]** and **[[Shogunate of Investigation]]**, burning research sites and releasing experimental creatures back into the wild.

---

## **Methods**

- Freeing captured or modified animals from research sites.
- Burning laboratories, convoys, and observation towers.
- Using controlled forest growth to trap and destroy facilities.
- Releasing toxins or fungi that reclaim industrial zones.
- Ignoring or abandoning human captives in the process.

The group does not care about human loss unless it affects an animal directly. They avoid cities and rarely attack civilian settlements, but researchers, hunters, and smugglers entering protected zones are marked for death.

---

## **Organization**

The Protectors have no central leadership. Small cells act independently, communicating through carved wooden idols and animal messengers. Their most feared leader, known only as **The Rootspeaker**, is said to commune with the minds of beasts and command the forests themselves.

---

## **Reputation**

To druids of the **[[Shogunate of Nature]]**, they are zealots who have forgotten balance.  
To the **[[Shogunate of Arcana]]**, they are dangerous fanatics who destroy research and infrastructure.  
To animals, forests, and the wilderness itself, they are defenders who never stopped fighting.
