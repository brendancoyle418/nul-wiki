The **Alchemists** are the scientific branch of the **[[Confederacy of the Slayers]]**, aligned with the **[[Shogunate of History]]**. Their purpose is to study the natural world through observation, experimentation, and recordkeeping. They believe that understanding matter and energy is the key to rebuilding the world after the **[[Sealing]]**.

Alchemists work in laboratories, workshops, and excavation sites, studying everything from chemical reactions to the biology of post-Sealing life. They also document historical changes in the land, tracking how time, magic, and decay affect both living and nonliving material. Their research often combines physical science with remnants of ancient alchemy once practiced before the gods were destroyed.

Alchemists are responsible for maintaining the Confederacy’s archives of natural knowledge. They share discoveries with **[[Mages]]** to refine magic, and with **[[Druids]]** to improve environmental recovery efforts. While they do not practice faith, many see their study of nature as a form of reverence for what remains of creation.

---

## **Roles and Practices**

- Conduct experiments on elements, minerals, and living organisms.
- Study how magic interacts with physical matter.
- Preserve historical samples and fossils from the time before the Sealing.
- Develop practical inventions for agriculture, medicine, and construction.
- Teach scientific methods to the other shogunates.

Alchemists value precision and preservation. They see the world as a system of reactions that can be understood and improved if studied with patience.
