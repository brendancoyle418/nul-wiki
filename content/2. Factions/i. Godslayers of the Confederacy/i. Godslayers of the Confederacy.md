The **Godslayers of the Confederacy** are the five major groups that make up the governing and cultural foundation of the **[[Confederacy of the Slayers]]**. Each group represents one of the Shogunates and has its own area of study and responsibility. Together they maintain order, stability, and progress in the dark world that followed the **[[Sealing]]**.

The Godslayers were formed to prevent divine power from returning. Their goal is to guide the world through reason, study, and cooperation instead of faith in gods. Each group approaches this goal in a different way, but all share the belief that mortals must shape their own future.

---

## **The Five Orders**

### **Alchemists (History)**

Alchemists study the natural world through experiment and observation. They seek to understand matter, energy, and life by uncovering the rules that govern them. Their work connects to the **[[Shogunate of History]]**, where they preserve knowledge, research physical law, and record changes in the world. Many serve as scholars, engineers, and researchers.

---

### **Druids (Nature)**

Druids maintain the natural world and help the other shogunates stay in balance. They often act as advisors, medics, and spiritual leaders, guiding both people and ecosystems toward harmony. They work closely with the **[[Shogunate of Nature]]**, restoring land damaged by conflict or corruption. Many still commune with elemental spirits that remain from before the Sealing.

---

### **Expositors (Investigation)**

Expositors oversee cooperation between the shogunates. They use psionics to communicate across great distances and to detect unrest or imbalance in the Confederacy. Their base is within the **[[Shogunate of Investigation]]**, where they maintain the tunnels and archives that connect all continents. They focus on logic, diplomacy, and investigation.

---

### **Mages (Arcana)**

Mages develop new forms of magic and refine old ones. They study the structure of spells and the flow of energy through the **[[Mass Mind]]**. Most work within the **[[Shogunate of Arcana]]**, where the Towers of Powder act as laboratories and relay points for magical study. Their goal is to make magic reliable and safe for all who use it.

---

### **Priests (Religion)**

Priests preserve the role of faith after the fall of the gods. They maintain old rituals and create new religions that focus on human values instead of divine ones. They work from the **[[Shogunate of Religion]]**, keeping the population united through shared belief and tradition. Priests act as teachers, counselors, and moral guides.

---

## **Role in the Confederacy**

Each order supports the others. Alchemists and Mages push scientific and magical understanding forward. Druids keep both nature and society stable. Expositors handle coordination, ensuring that information and resources flow smoothly. Priests maintain cultural identity and belief, giving purpose to the Confederacy’s people. Together, they are what keeps the mortal world alive and self-sustaining in the endless night.
