**Reality** within the **[[Mortal Plane]]** is divided into five layered states of being known as the **Planes of Existence**. Each plane represents a fundamental aspect of reality: **matter**, **spirit**, and **soul**, and describes how these forces interact to sustain all life and structure.

All things are formed from **matter**, given identity through **spirit**, and guided by **soul** where awareness exists. Objects, plants, and landscapes possess only matter and spirit, living passively within the rhythm of creation. Beings capable of reflection and intention (+3 Intelligence or higher) contain all three, their souls serving as a bridge between individuality and the collective consciousness of the world.

These five planes describe how existence functions, from the foundation of elements to the union of all thought.

---

### **i. Constant Plane**

The **Constant Plane** is the highest and most complete layer of existence. It is the realm of **souls**, the collective consciousness that binds all living awareness together. Every soul originates from and ultimately returns to the Constant, where individuality dissolves into unity. It is the total memory of the world—the infinite archive of all who have lived, thought, or felt.

**[[Psionics]]** draws from this plane. Psions reach into the shared field of the Constant to touch the combined awareness of every soul, both living and departed. Through alignment with this vast mind, they act through resonance and intention rather than physical force. The Constant Plane is unity given thought, the source and destination of all consciousness.

---

### **ii. Astral Plane**

The **Astral Plane** is the realm of **individual soul** and will. It preserves selfhood and emotion, allowing sentient beings to express identity and choice. The Astral connects all thinking life to the greater consciousness of the Constant, yet maintains the boundaries that define individuality. It is an ocean of dreams, thought, and potential through which awareness moves.

**[[Magic]]** functions through the Astral. Mages reshape reality by altering its Astral reflection, which the lower planes then manifest. When a soul dies, its awareness passes through the Astral, shedding form and memory before merging again with the Constant.

---

### **iii. Material Plane**

The **Material Plane** is the realm of **matter**, the physical layer of existence where energy and form coexist. It is finite, visible, and stable, maintained by the **[[Catalytic Spheres]]** following the **[[Sealing]]**. All measurable reality belongs here, from mountains to oceans to living bodies.

**[[Alchemy]]** studies the Material Plane as the interaction of matter and spirit under natural law. Alchemists learn to transform materials through reaction, never creation or destruction, following the truth that nothing is ever lost—only changed. The Material Plane is the center of existence, where soul and spirit meet within form.

---

### **iv. Ethereal Plane**

The **Ethereal Plane** is the realm of **spirit**, the invisible pattern that defines identity and shape. Every object and being has an ethereal form that determines how it exists within matter. Without spirit, matter would lose its boundary and collapse into formless essence.

**[[Chi]]** operates through the Ethereal Plane. Practitioners guide the motion of spirit, called _flow_ or _breath_, to strengthen, heal, and balance the world. Through mastery of this current, life remains coherent and aligned with its intended form.

---

### **v. Elemental Planes**

The **Elemental Planes** lie beneath the Ethereal, forming the infinite foundation of **spirit and form**. They are not static worlds but living domains shaped by **spirits** and **unselfs** that define the behavior of each element across reality. Every concept, material, and natural force has an associated plane: Fire, Stone, Blood, Smoke, Carbon, Mercury, and many others. Each is both habitat and principle, giving the material world its properties.

**[[Druidcraft]]** draws from these planes. Druids commune directly with elemental spirits, serving as intermediaries between the natural world and its foundations. Through this connection, they restore balance where elemental flows are disrupted. When a being dies, its spirit returns to its corresponding element, joining the vast cycle of renewal and dissolution.
