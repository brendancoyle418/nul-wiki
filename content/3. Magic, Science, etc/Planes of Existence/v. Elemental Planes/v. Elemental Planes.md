**Elemental Planes** are infinite realms of pure spirit, existing beyond the **[[Ethereal Plane]]**. Each plane is a perfect embodiment of a single substance, force, or concept, formed and sustained by the collective nature of the spirits that inhabit it. These planes serve as the source of all spiritual identity, defining how existence behaves in the **[[Material Plane]]** through resonance rather than direct contact.

Every Elemental Plane is shaped by the instinct and pattern of its spirits. The **Plane of Carbon** grows as a dark forest of crystal lattices, expanding and fracturing in endless precision. The **Plane of Oxygen** moves as a sea of invisible flame, where motion and pressure create an eternal, consuming dance. The **Plane of Iron** vibrates like a living metal, its surface shifting with rhythmic pulses that echo through the spiritual field. There are planes for every essence ever known—ash, blood, salt, smoke, decay, and countless more—each forming one part of the world’s unseen structure.

Spirits within these planes do not think or remember. They exist as the pure expression of their element, guided by instinct rather than awareness. A spirit of Fire is not a creature made of flame but the act of burning itself. Within the Elemental Planes, this unity between being and function is complete. There is no separation between what something is and what it does.

**[[Druidcraft]]** draws directly from these planes. Druids do not command the elements but commune with the spirits that define them. Through resonance, they invite harmony between the spiritual and the living. Fire is encouraged to warm rather than consume, water to flow rather than stagnate, and stone to hold rather than resist. This is not magic but negotiation, a restoration of balance between the world and the elemental spirits that sustain it.

The Elemental Planes are infinite and eternal. They are not distant places but reflections of what already exists in all things. Every flame, gust, and heartbeat is an echo of its greater source. The **[[Ethereal Plane]]** bridges these realms, carrying the movement of spirit from the elements into living form and back again. When the balance of the Elemental Planes is disturbed, the Material weakens, and the order of life begins to fail.
