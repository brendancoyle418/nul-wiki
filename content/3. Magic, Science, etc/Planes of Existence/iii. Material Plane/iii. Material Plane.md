The **Material Plane** is the realm of **matter and form**, the physical foundation of all existence. It contains all worlds that can be touched and measured, including the **[[Mortal Plane]]**, which represents only a small portion of its total expanse. Every mountain, sea, and living body is built from the same base matter that defines this plane. The Material does not think or feel; it simply exists, shaped by whatever forces act upon it.

Since the **[[Sealing]]**, the Material Plane has become finite, a closed system bound by its own limits. Within it, the **[[Catalytic Spheres]]** maintain the laws of matter and motion, preventing the world from collapsing into formless dust. These spheres generate the elemental patterns that make life possible and ensure that every physical thing maintains its shape and weight.

The Material is also the home of **[[Alchemy]]**, the art of understanding and transforming matter. Alchemists view this plane as the most direct expression of reality, where every reaction reveals the structure of creation itself. While magic and chi draw from the **[[Astral Plane]]** and **[[Ethereal Plane]]**, alchemy works purely within the Material, guiding how energy, substance, and change interact.

The Material Plane is layered and interconnected with all others. The **[[Astral Plane]]** presses upon it through the influence of souls, shaping emotion and identity. The **[[Ethereal Plane]]** breathes through it as spirit, giving matter movement and form. Beyond these lie the **[[Elemental Planes]]**, infinite reflections of substance and composition that define what matter can become. Every object, creature, and landscape is a point of intersection where these forces meet.

Though it feels stable, the Material Plane is always in motion. Continents shift, minerals form and dissolve, and living things decay back into the dust that once formed them. It is both the origin and the grave of all creation, the stage upon which every other plane leaves its mark.
