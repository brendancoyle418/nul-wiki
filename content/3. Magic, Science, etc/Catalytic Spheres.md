**The Catalytic Spheres** are six massive orbs suspended above the **[[Big Fucking Pit]]**, each regulating a core system of reality across the **[[Mortal Plane]]**. They appeared in **Year 10**, following the disappearance of the **[[Shogunate of Nature]]** during the **[[Planetary Ascension]]**, and have remained in perfect orbit ever since. Their arrangement defines the balance of energy, matter, and time, ensuring the world remains stable after the **[[Sealing]]**.

Each Sphere sustains a fundamental property of existence—light, energy, matter, balance, time, and nullification. Together, they keep the sealed world from collapsing in on itself. The Spheres are monitored from fortified bases built by each **[[Shogunate]]** along the Pit’s descending walls, where resonance levels are strongest.

---

## **The Six Spheres**

### **Sphere I – White Sphere**

The **white sphere** emits neutral light that defines color, visibility, and clarity across the world. It maintains illumination through the eternal night, allowing the Mortal Plane to remain perceivable. Its influence is constant and even, touching every surface equally.

---

### **Sphere II – Red Spheres**

The **twin red spheres** generate positive and negative energy, creating arcs of lightning between them. They maintain charge, motion, and polarity across the planet, regulating flow in both natural and artificial systems. Together they represent power in balance—every action matched by equal return.

---

### **Sphere III – Blue Spheres**

The **three blue spheres** preserve the states of matter: solid, liquid, and gas. Their resonance governs phase transitions, temperature thresholds, and atmospheric stability. Without their influence, matter would lose cohesion and the seas and sky would dissolve into chaos.

---

### **Sphere IV – Green Spheres**

The **four green spheres** control environmental conditions—heat, cold, moisture, and dryness. Their combined movement maintains the planetary cycle of weather and climate, ensuring natural equilibrium between growth and decay.

---

### **Sphere V – Yellow Spheres**

The **five yellow spheres** regulate temporal flow: the distant past, past, present, future, and distant future. Their synchronized orbit ensures that time progresses consistently across the Mortal Plane. Disturbances near them often result in temporal drift or localized time fractures.

---

### **Sphere VI – Purple Spheres**

The **six purple spheres** serve as nullifiers for the previous five, orbiting around a central **nul sphere**. They absorb and stabilize excess energy, pressure, or distortion from the others, preventing collapse or overload. The Vacuum spheres act as failsafes, maintaining the limits of existence itself.

---

## **Function and Observation**

- The Spheres remain in perfect alignment, unaffected by gravity or magnetic fields.
- Their glow never fades, yet produces no measurable heat or radiation.
- No known force can alter their orbit, and no expedition has reached their surfaces.
- Resonance from their activity sustains every form of magic, life, and stability across the Mortal Plane.

The Catalytic Spheres are the last known work of the vanished **[[Shogunate of Nature]]**, a planetary mechanism designed to keep the sealed world alive.
