**Spirit** is the essence of **form and motion**, the unseen current that gives shape and life to the world through the **[[Ethereal Plane]]**. It defines what things are and how they behave, linking all forms of matter in a single flow of movement and change. Where the **[[Soul]]** provides awareness and choice, the spirit provides identity and pattern. Without spirit, matter would collapse into lifeless substance, and the world would lose the structure that holds it together.

The **Ethereal Plane** is the natural home of spirit. It moves like a great ocean of light and rhythm, carrying the patterns that connect the **[[Material Plane]]** to the **[[Elemental Planes]]**. Through this flow, spirit gives life to all things and returns energy to the elements that sustain them. The motion of spirit between these planes creates the cycle of growth, decay, and renewal that defines existence.

Every spirit follows a pattern that reflects its nature. The spirit of fire burns, the spirit of water flows, the spirit of stone endures, and the spirit of air moves freely. These behaviors are not learned but are expressions of what each spirit is. The spirits of living beings are more complex, influenced by instinct, memory, and emotion. A single creature may contain many spirits working together, each tied to a part of the body or mind, maintaining the harmony that keeps life in balance.

Spirits are not self-aware. They act through resonance and instinct, responding to emotion, memory, and energy. When disrupted or corrupted, their motion becomes unstable, leading to imbalance, decay, or death. Practitioners of **[[Chi]]** study the flow of spirit within the body, guiding and repairing it when it falters. Through calm awareness and breath, they restore harmony between body, mind, and environment.

**[[Druidcraft]]** works by communing directly with spirits in the natural world. Druids reach through the **[[Catalytic Spheres]]** to the **[[Elemental Planes]]**, where pure spirits of flame, stone, wind, and growth exist in their truest forms. They do not command these spirits but speak with them, reminding each of its purpose and balance. By honoring this connection, druids restore forests, calm storms, and heal the land. Their art is an act of cooperation, not domination.

When a living creature dies, its spirit unbinds from the **[[Material Plane]]** and returns to the Ethereal. Over time, it drifts into the **[[Elemental Planes]]**, dissolving into the essence that first gave it life. This return maintains the balance of creation. Only those trapped by pain, obsession, or corruption remain behind as ghosts, unable to rejoin the cycle.

Spirit is the quiet rhythm beneath all existence. It is the breath of the world, the structure of life, and the current that carries all things from creation to return. Through its endless motion, the planes remain whole, and the world endures.
