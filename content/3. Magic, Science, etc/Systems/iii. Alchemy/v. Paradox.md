**Paradox** is the fifth alchemical catalyst, drawn from the **[[Yellow Spheres]]**. It governs instability through temporal overlap, forcing materials to exist in multiple states of potential and form. Paradox represents interference between moments, allowing one version of a substance to interact with another within the same space. It does not create new matter but alters existing material by folding separate outcomes together.

In alchemy, Paradox reactions use suspended timefields to create overlapping conditions within a mixture. Reagents are guided through repeating intervals where their past and future states converge. This process can produce compounds with conflicting qualities, such as stone that flows like liquid or metal that shifts between weight and lightness. Even a small error in rhythm can cause the reaction to collapse or dissolve entirely.

Gold is the material most closely tied to Paradox. It resists corrosion, distortion, and temporal strain better than any other known medium. Alchemists use gold lines, seals, and lattices to bind unstable matter and prevent it from unraveling across timelines. Its endurance provides an anchor that holds the rest of the experiment together while the reagents fluctuate between states.

Paradox can be seen naturally in repeating or regenerative cycles such as the shedding of bark, the return of seasons, or the rhythm of tides. To alchemists, it is the study of interference and correction, a tool that reveals how time can be bent without breaking the continuity of the world.
