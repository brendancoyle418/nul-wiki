**Primal Magic** is the manipulation of natural force through the **[[Green Spheres]]**, which maintain the balance of the elements across the **[[Mortal Plane]]**. It is the most instinctive and least structured form of casting, relying on the caster’s awareness of motion, pressure, and life rather than deliberate study. Practitioners act as extensions of the world itself, moving in rhythm with the natural cycles that the Green Spheres sustain.

The **Green Spheres** govern the four elemental states—fire, water, earth, and air—and serve as the foundation of all environmental change. Apprentices begin by channeling through these spheres to sense how the elements move and react. Over time, they learn to adjust their flow without direct focus, aligning themselves with the natural pulse of the world rather than shaping it by force.

A primal caster’s **Astral Body** manifests within the **[[Astral Plane]]** as one of four wandering forms: **Meteors** for Fire, **Comets** for Water, **Asteroids** for Earth, and **Satellites** for Air. These celestial bodies drift freely through the void, reflecting the caster’s attunement to change, gravity, and momentum. Advanced practitioners learn to draw energy from their Astral form directly, guiding its movement to influence the surrounding environment.

Primal Magic is used for shaping terrain, directing weather, and reinforcing the connection between life and the elements. It is a tool of growth, preservation, and survival. Unlike other disciplines, it does not separate the caster from nature but allows them to act as part of it, carrying the same motion that governs all living and elemental things.
