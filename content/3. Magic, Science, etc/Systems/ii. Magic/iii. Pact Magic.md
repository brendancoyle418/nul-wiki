**Pact Magic** is the practice of channeling energy through mutual exchange, built on the structure of the **[[Blue Spheres]]**. These spheres represent the balance between giver, receiver, and the bond that connects them. Pact Magic relies on this triad, creating a stable flow of energy between linked souls. It is not forced control but cooperation, where power is shared rather than commanded.

Each pact forms a living circuit between the **patron**, the **pact**, and the **practitioner**. The Blue Spheres act as the original medium for this connection, teaching mages how to sustain magical energy through mutual resonance. Beginners use the Spheres to anchor and stabilize their bond, ensuring that energy does not collapse or consume one side of the link.

A pact practitioner’s **Astral Body** manifests as a **Constellation** within the **[[Astral Plane]]**, a network of connected stars that reflect their active bonds. Each point represents a relationship or agreement, glowing brighter as the exchange strengthens and fading when the bond weakens. Advanced mages learn to form these constellations without the Spheres, drawing directly from their own Astral links to maintain power across distance or planes.

Pact Magic is used for summoning, binding, communication, and shared empowerment. It allows one soul to act through another, extending abilities or knowledge through the established pact. In the modern age, most practitioners treat the art as both magical and contractual, ensuring precise terms to prevent imbalance or loss of autonomy.
