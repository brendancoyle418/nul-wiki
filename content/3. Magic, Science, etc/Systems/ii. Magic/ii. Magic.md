**Magic** is the practice of drawing power from the **[[Astral Plane]]**, where every soul exists as a celestial body—stars, planets, comets, or voids drifting through infinite space. A spell is an act of gravity between planes, pulling from one’s own Astral body to influence the **[[Material Plane]]**. The **[[Catalytic Spheres]]** once served as bridges to this energy, but as mages advance, they learn to cast directly through the power of their own soul.

The form of a caster’s Astral body determines how their magic behaves. Divine mages radiate like suns, while shadow mages collapse like black holes. These forms are not chosen but revealed, shaped by the mage’s nature and the type of magic they practice. Over time, the body and the Astral form grow linked, each movement of the soul reflected as change in the physical world.

---

## **Branches of Magic**

### **Divine Magic** _(White Sphere)_

Divine mages manifest as **Astral Suns**, radiant bodies of order and illumination. Their magic shapes raw energy into form, granting clarity, warmth, and protection. Each act of Divine magic is an act of structure, using faith or purpose to bring definition to chaos.

---

### **Arcane Magic** _(Red Spheres)_

Arcane mages become **Astral Planets**, precise bodies that store and release energy in cycles. Their power relies on pattern, focus, and control. Every spell begins with charge and ends in balance. Arcane practitioners embody logic and structure, turning the Astral into a system of motion and cause.

---

### **Pact Magic** _(Blue Spheres)_

Pact mages form **Astral Constellations**, networks of light bound by mutual exchange. Each connection represents a pact between souls—patron, pact, and practitioner. The constellation glows brighter when balanced and flickers when bonds weaken. Their strength depends on cooperation, not domination.

---

### **Primal Magic** _(Green Spheres)_

Primal mages take the form of **Astral Comets, Meteors, Asteroids, and Satellites**, fragments of creation that move freely through the void. They draw upon the raw elements of fire, water, earth, and air. Their power is instinctive, driven by emotion and will rather than training or study.

---

### **Wild Magic** _(Yellow Spheres)_

Wild mages appear as **Astral Nebulas**, vast clouds of color and light where past, present, and future mix. Their spells pull from chance and potential, breaking normal order. Wild magic changes the flow of the Astral itself, bending what is possible into what happens.

---

### **Shadow Magic** _(Purple Spheres)_

Shadow mages become **Astral Black Holes**, dense centers of silence that consume energy and form. Their magic erases, silences, and unravels, creating spaces of nothingness. They use absence as power, shaping stillness into strength.

---

## **Practice and Mastery**

Casting magic begins with drawing from one’s Astral body and directing that energy through will. The more a mage understands their Astral form, the greater their control becomes. Those who master this connection can act without aid from the **[[Catalytic Spheres]]**, shaping the world through their own cosmic reflection. At this point, the mage’s soul and spell are the same thing—the motion of a star made manifest.
