**Shadow Magic** is the manipulation of absence through the **[[Purple Spheres]]**, which regulate stillness and nullification across the **[[Mortal Plane]]**. It draws power from what is not: light uncast, sound unheard, and energy withdrawn from motion. Practitioners shape the gaps left between things rather than the things themselves, using silence and emptiness as tools. Shadow Magic does not destroy; it removes, conceals, and isolates, leaving behind only the trace of what once was.

The **Purple Spheres**, along with the central **nul sphere**, preserve the balance of void within creation. They prevent the excess of energy, motion, or time from overwhelming the world by anchoring it in stillness. Shadow mages channel this state of quiet balance, drawing strength from negation. Each spell is an act of subtraction that silences sound, dims light, or severs connection between planes.

A shadow mage’s **Astral Body** manifests as a **Black Hole** within the **[[Astral Plane]]**, a motionless core of gravity that pulls all nearby energy inward. It reflects mastery of containment and restraint. As their skill grows, shadow mages learn to draw directly from this void, shaping it to absorb or silence what stands before them. Casting requires precision; if control is lost, the void consumes both the spell and the caster.

Shadow Magic is often mistaken for destruction, but its purpose is balance. It is used to stabilize unstable energies, contain dangerous forces, or seal breaches between planes. Practitioners understand that emptiness is a state of structure, and silence is necessary for the world to hold its shape.
