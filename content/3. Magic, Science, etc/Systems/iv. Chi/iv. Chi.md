**Chi** is the study of spiritual balance through the **[[Ethereal Plane]]**, using the movement of the **[[Catalytic Spheres]]** as a guide to understanding the flow of life. Practitioners of chi focus on harmony between body and spirit, opening internal pathways known as **Gates** that align the organs with the cosmic rhythm of the spheres. Each Gate mirrors a fundamental principle of the world and connects the individual to one of the six forces that sustain the **[[Mortal Plane]]**.

The Spheres orbit above the **[[Big Fucking Pit]]**, but their influence extends into every living being. By attuning to their motion, practitioners learn to feel how chi moves through their own body and through all life around them. Opening the Gates strengthens that connection, allowing the body to purify, perceive, and transform through spiritual motion. Those who go beyond the six Gates walk the **[[Monastic Paths]]**, traditions that refine spirit until it acts independently of the body.

---

## **The Six Gates**

### **Gate of the Liver** _(White Sphere)_

The liver purifies both body and spirit. Practitioners who open this gate can cleanse corruption, purge disease, and restore balance within themselves or others. The Gate of the Liver is associated with clarity and renewal, mirroring the white sphere’s steady radiance that restores harmony to the world.

---

### **Gate of the Heart** _(Red Spheres)_

The heart governs rhythm and polarity. When open, it allows chi to flow evenly, balancing vitality and calm. Practitioners use it to harmonize emotion, temper aggression, and stabilize the body’s pulse. Its connection to the red spheres gives it control over action and stillness, as well as life and rest.

---

### **Gate of the Lungs** _(Blue Spheres)_

The lungs control exchange—between breath and silence, motion and rest. This gate teaches mastery of energy through breath, aligning chi with the natural flow of air and pressure. The blue spheres guide this gate’s rhythm, linking it to the balance between endurance, stillness, and release.

---

### **Gate of the Brain** _(Green Spheres)_

The brain orders perception and consciousness. When opened, this gate allows thoughts to flow without obstruction and dreams to merge with waking awareness. Its bond with the green spheres reflects its purpose: to balance the internal and external worlds, keeping the mind steady across change.

---

### **Gate of the Eyes** _(Yellow Spheres)_

The eyes serve as the link between perception and truth. Opening this gate sharpens sight across all five planes—**[[Material]]**, **[[Astral]]**, **[[Ethereal]]**, **[[Elemental]]**, and **[[Constant]]**—allowing one to see the motion of chi directly. The yellow spheres guide its rhythm, tying vision to time and memory.

---

### **Gate of the Stomach** _(Purple Spheres)_

The stomach transforms what is taken in, mirroring the nullifying power of the purple spheres. It converts pain, hunger, or poison into fuel for endurance. Practitioners who master this gate can redirect harm into strength, consuming imbalance and restoring equilibrium to themselves and others.

---

## **Practice and Purpose**

Chi is neither power nor magic. It is discipline through awareness, the still current that carries all life. Practitioners learn by listening to their breath and feeling the motion of the spheres, moving their spirit in rhythm with the world’s heartbeat. The Six Gates are only the beginning—beyond them lie the Monastic Paths, where body and spirit act as one.
