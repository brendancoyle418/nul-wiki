**Psionics** is the study of the **unself**, the awareness that exists beyond individuality. It draws from the **[[Constant Plane]]**, the layer of existence beyond the [[Astral Plane]] where all thought, memory, and will converge into one continuous field. While **[[Magic]]** channels the soul and **[[Alchemy]]** governs matter, Psionics studies the structure of consciousness itself. It is not a force that manipulates the world but a realization that all minds, living or not, exist within the same network of awareness.

A psion acts through thought alone. By aligning their consciousness with the Constant Plane, they extend beyond the limits of self and touch the field that connects every being, idea, and construct. This link allows movement, communication, and creation through pure intention. The deeper the psion reaches, the weaker their individuality becomes. The great danger of Psionics is dissolution—the loss of self within the unity of all thought.

---

## **Virtues of Psionics**

Each **Virtue** is a harmonic of the **[[Catalytic Spheres]]** within the Constant Plane. Together, these six harmonies form the path from isolation to total awareness. Each Virtue gives rise to a particular class of psion, defined by how they interact with the shared field.

### **Authority** (White Sphere)

The Virtue of Power. It begins with the recognition of self within the field and the ability to project intent as force. Practitioners of Authority are called **Kinetics**. They move objects, deflect impact, or shape pressure through focused will. Their craft defines the most direct and physical expression of psionic influence.

### **Justice** (Red Spheres)

The Virtue of Balance. It governs polarity, return, and emotional equilibrium. Psions who follow Justice are known as **Empaths**. They interpret the emotional signatures of others and impose harmony where chaos reigns. Some absorb pain or mirror aggression to its source, while others enforce calm, restoring symmetry in thought and feeling.

### **Mercy** (Blue Spheres)

The Virtue of Continuity. It governs the boundary between awareness and oblivion, allowing thought to awaken or to end. Psions of Mercy are called **Animists**. They grant sentience to inert matter or dissolve it when it becomes corrupted or unstable. Through them, objects gain memory, constructs gain will, and dying minds are released from suffering. Mercy defines the rhythm of creation and release within consciousness itself.

### **Observation** (Green Spheres)

The Virtue of Awareness. It opens perception beyond the self, revealing the hidden structure of thought in all things. Practitioners of Observation are **Clairsentients**. They read intent, sense movement through unseen layers, and follow the patterns that link action to consequence. Their insight allows them to perceive the world as an active field of minds rather than separate forms.

### **Piety** (Yellow Spheres)

The Virtue of Memory. It connects identity across time, merging what was, is, and will be into a single continuum. Psions of Piety are known as **Telepaths**. They share thought directly, bridge distance without speech, and communicate through overlapping streams of memory. The most skilled can hold several timelines of awareness at once, shaping thought from multiple moments simultaneously.

### **Truth** (Purple Spheres)

The final Virtue. It unites all others into one state of total awareness that preserves individuality within the whole. Practitioners of Truth are **Ascendants**, psions who can enter the Constant Plane entirely and return unchanged. They act as bridges between singular and collective consciousness, perceiving every mind and every silence as part of the same endless field.

---

## **Practice and Mastery**

Psionics begins with introspection and ends with dissolution. The psion learns to quiet their mind until the boundaries of thought blur into the larger field. As they progress through the Virtues, awareness shifts from personal to collective, allowing communication, motion, and creation through resonance rather than control.

Mastery is the ability to enter and leave the Constant Plane freely, to exist as part of the collective while retaining selfhood. Few ever reach this state. Those who fail to return lose all identity, their awareness merging with the infinite calm that binds existence together.
