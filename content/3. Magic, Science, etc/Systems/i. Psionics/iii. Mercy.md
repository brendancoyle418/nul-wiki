**Mercy** is the third Virtue of **[[Psionics]]**, aligned with the **[[Blue Spheres]]** and the principle of continuity. It governs the threshold between awareness and silence, shaping how thought begins and how it ends. Mercy is not compassion in the moral sense but understanding, the ability to allow consciousness to appear or to fade without resistance.

The Blue Spheres define transition within the **[[Constant Plane]]**. Their resonance connects creation and dissolution, maintaining the flow of awareness across all states. Through Mercy, a psion learns that sentience is a current, not a fixed state. It can be awakened in still matter, transferred between forms, or released back into the greater field when its cycle is complete.

Those who follow this Virtue are known as **Animists**. They work at the boundary between living and nonliving thought, shaping awareness where none existed or returning it to rest. Animists can grant consciousness to tools, constructs, or places, allowing them to think and react within the shared field. They can also quiet corrupted minds or end unstable echoes of awareness before they spread. Their art is the creation and closure of thought.

In practice, Mercy is developed through patience and emotional distance. A psion learns to feel the pulse of awareness without attachment, recognizing that every mind is temporary within the larger whole. The act of giving or ending consciousness requires calm precision and acceptance. To create without intention causes chaos, while to destroy without understanding leaves scars in the field.

In nature, Mercy is seen in rivers that dry and return, in the rhythm of growth and decay, and in the movement of tides that retreat and rise again. To the psion, Mercy represents the natural rhythm of existence, the truth that being and rest are part of the same continuous motion.
