**Chronomancy** is the study of time through the **[[Yellow Spheres]]**, which govern the five layers of temporal flow: the distant past, past, present, future, and distant future. It is one of the most fragile forms of **[[Druidcraft]]**, since time is not a material to shape but a structure that holds all motion together. Chronomancy teaches understanding through rhythm and pattern, allowing a druid to adjust how they or others experience the passing of moments without disturbing the greater order.

The Yellow Spheres maintain the sequence of cause and effect across the **[[Mortal Plane]]**. Druids who study them learn to shift their awareness between moments, lengthening perception or shortening reaction, seeing how every action fits into a larger design. Time cannot be stopped or reversed, only bent slightly in how it is felt. Too much interference risks disconnection, leaving the caster lost between moments.

Chronomancers describe time as a vast lattice of interlocking gears, each turn influencing countless others. The present is the gear they stand upon, while the past and future are the machinery that surrounds it. Every motion follows another, and even the smallest adjustment sends vibrations through the entire system. The art of Chronomancy is knowing which gears to touch and when to leave them turning.

Those who practice this discipline are patient and deliberate. Many serve as scholars or historians, preserving knowledge so that others remember what the world forgets. Others act as mediators, sensing the timing of conflict and resolution. To them, wisdom lies in moving with the machine rather than against it.

To study Chronomancy is to listen for the ticking of the world and understand that time is not a river or a weapon but a structure. It turns endlessly, and those who walk with it learn when to act, when to wait, and when to remain still.
