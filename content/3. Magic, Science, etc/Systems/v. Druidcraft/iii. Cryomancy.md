
The Blue Spheres maintain the structure of all things, defining how matter holds together and when it should yield. Druids who follow this path learn to work with these transitions, controlling temperature and density to shape their surroundings. A cryomancer may freeze a river, turn mist into crystal, or liquefy stone by aligning with the natural balance between states.

Cryomancers are often found in glacial regions or deep caves where change is slow and deliberate. Their rituals involve long periods of focus and silence, mirroring the calm needed to move between states without breaking them. To them, ice is not lifeless but waiting, a form of matter that remembers its movement even while still.

The art teaches that every material carries the memory of what it was and what it will become. Water remembers vapor, and stone remembers dust. A skilled Cryomancer can speak to these memories, guiding matter to take on new forms without destroying its essence.

To study Cryomancy is to learn control through restraint. It is not about stopping movement but about slowing it enough to understand it. The Blue Spheres do not destroy or create; they preserve the space between both.
