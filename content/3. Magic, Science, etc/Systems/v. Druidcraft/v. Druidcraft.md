**Druidcraft** is the study of nature through direct contact with the **[[Elemental Planes]]**, which exist beyond the **[[Ethereal Plane]]**. Druids use the **[[Catalytic Spheres]]** as gateways to communicate with these planes, reaching the raw matter and energy that shape the **[[Mortal Plane]]**. Their goal is not control but balance, working with natural forces instead of forcing change.

Each of the six Spheres connects to a specific part of the world’s structure: light, energy, matter, environment, time, and stillness. By focusing through a Sphere, a druid can reach its corresponding elements and influence them on a small scale. The practice relies on rhythm and respect—druids guide what already exists rather than summoning or creating from nothing.

---

## **Disciplines of Druidcraft**

### **Pyromancy** _(White Sphere)_

Pyromancy uses the **White Sphere**, the source of light and heat. Druids who follow this path can ignite fire without fuel, control illumination, and burn away corruption. The art focuses on renewal through flame and the natural cycle of destruction and growth.

---

### **Hemomancy** _(Red Spheres)_

Hemomancy channels the **Red Spheres**, which control positive and negative energy. This path deals with life, motion, and decay. Practitioners use it to heal wounds, drain vitality, or balance energy in living things. It treats blood and lightning as part of the same current.

---

### **Cryomancy** _(Blue Spheres)_

Cryomancy draws from the **Blue Spheres**, which maintain solids, liquids, and gases. A cryomancer can freeze, liquefy, or vaporize matter by shifting it between natural states. This discipline emphasizes structure and patience—nothing is truly destroyed, only changed.

---

### **Geomancy** _(Green Spheres)_

Geomancy connects to the **Green Spheres**, which regulate climate and environment. Druids use it to shape land, alter weather, and balance the conditions that allow life to exist. It is the most practical and widespread discipline, often tied to farming, healing, and construction.

---

### **Chronomancy** _(Yellow Spheres)_

Chronomancy follows the **Yellow Spheres**, which govern the five layers of time. It allows short-term adjustments to motion and perception—slowing, speeding, or sensing the flow of time around the druid. It cannot rewrite history, only alter how moments are experienced.

---

### **Necromancy** _(Purple Spheres)_

Necromancy uses the **Purple Spheres** and the central **nul sphere**, which stabilize death and stillness. Practitioners guide spirits, communicate with the dead, and manage the passage between the **[[Material Plane]]** and the **[[Ethereal Plane]]**. It focuses on understanding the boundary between life and death, not breaking it.

---

## **Practice and Purpose**

Druidcraft depends on awareness and restraint. The Spheres act as channels that let druids reach the elemental forces keeping the world stable. Their role is to keep that balance intact, using the same tools that maintain the planet itself. Where others see magic as power, druids see it as cooperation.
